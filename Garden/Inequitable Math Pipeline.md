# Inequitable Math Pipeline

> [!abstract] Problem Statement
> Standard engineering courses treat incoming mathematical fluency as a proxy for raw intelligence. Students from underfunded public high schools, adult learners returning after years in the workforce, and veterans transitioning out of service are often written off on day one, mistaking an uneven educational background for a lack of engineering talent.

> [!tip] Measurable Goals
> Eliminate early-semester math panic for students with varying levels of math confidence, ensuring 100% of students experience the course as a growth pipeline where effort builds skill, regardless of prior math background.

---

### Course Solutions & Policies

* **Knewton Alta Mastery + 4-Hour Time Option:** Students earn 100% credit on homework by reaching 70% mastery OR by investing 4 active work hours. Raw effort directly offsets uneven secondary preparation.
* **"Tony's Intertwingled Memex" Prerequisite Support:** Curated, hyper-linked prerequisite support materials and visual guides hosted on the course website for instant, just-in-time concept reinforcement and skill refinement.
* **Forward-Looking GA-Led Discussion Sessions:** Friday Graduate Assistant-led discussions focus specifically on reviewing prerequisite mathematical skills required for the upcoming week's core engineering content.
