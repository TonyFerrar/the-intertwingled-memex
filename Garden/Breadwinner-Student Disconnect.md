# Breadwinner-Student Disconnect

> [!abstract] Problem Statement
> UNLV engineers routinely balance 30-hour resort or casino graveyard shifts, contribute directly to household rent, or care for family members. Traditional course policies treat a student covering a last-minute work shift or managing a family emergency as "lazy" or "uncommitted," mistaking incredible real-world resilience for academic indifference.

> [!tip] Measurable Goals
> Provide a predictable operational framework grounded in "rigorous empathy"—where working students can successfully balance 30-hour graveyard shifts and family duties without sacrificing mastery, missing deadlines, or needing to beg for excuses.

---

### Course Solutions & Policies

* **7-Day Rolling Grace Period:** Knewton Alta automatically allows late homework completion for 100% full credit up to 7 days past the target due date.
* **Knewton 4-Hour Request Form:** A simple 0-point Canvas form for students who log 4+ hours on an assignment during heavy work weeks to receive a manual TA credit override.
* **Canvas Automatic Drops:** System automatically drops the 2 lowest homework scores at the end of the semester, absorbing acute life emergencies without requiring employer or medical documentation.
