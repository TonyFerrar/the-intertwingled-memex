# Veteran and Non-Traditional Misalignment

> [!abstract] Problem Statement
> Veterans, adult learners, and parents bring real-world operational discipline and risk management into the classroom. Yet standard higher education micro-manages them with infantilizing attendance rules and rigid busywork, treating adult professionals like high schoolers while ignoring the cultural gaps they face fitting in with younger classmates.

> [!tip] Measurable Goals
> Bridge the social and cultural gap between adult learners and younger peers, leveraging veterans' and non-traditional students' real-world discipline as active classroom leadership while respecting their adult autonomy and external schedules.

---

### Course Solutions & Policies

* **Structured Group Problem-Solving:** Pairs adult learners' operational discipline with younger peers during 75-minute sessions, fostering mutual respect, team cohesion, and organic peer networking.
* **Predictable Operational Calendar:** Fixed Mon/Wed target due dates and automatic 7-day hard locks respect external family, professional, and military commitments.
* **Advance Material Publishing:** All assignments, lecture notes, and active learning materials are published far in advance and *never* later than 24 hours before class, allowing non-traditional students to schedule study blocks around work and family.
