# Evaluation Anxiety and Systemic Failure Paralysis

> [!abstract] Problem Statement
> High-stakes testing and punitive grading systems induce extreme grade anxiety, driving students toward paralysis, panic-cheating, or abandoning courses after an early failure rather than learning from mistakes.

> [!tip] Measurable Goals
> Normalize failure as an essential diagnostic step in the engineering learning cycle, drastically reduce grade anxiety, and provide clear, objective, self-directed recovery pathways.

---

### Course Solutions & Policies

* **Retroactive Grade Replacement:** Final exam score replaces the lowest midterm score if the student attempts (non-zero score) at least 80% of assigned homework sets and completes exam corrections.
* **Review Center Homework Recovery:** Complete 8 active hours in the Knewton Test Review Center (4 hours per assignment) to replace up to 2 lowest homework scores in a unit with a 100%.
* **Self-Auditing Canvas Quiz:** A 0-point, unlimited-attempt Canvas quiz allows students to independently check retro-grading eligibility without asking permission or feeling awkward.
* **Exam Correction Pathways:** Structured post-mortems done in office hours transform midterm mistakes into deep conceptual learning and reclaimed eligibility.
