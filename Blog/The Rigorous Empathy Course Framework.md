# The Rigorous Empathy Course Framework

Traditional higher education was designed around an outdated, 19th-century elite archetype. At a modern metropolitan university like UNLV, students face systemic hurdles that standard university policies fail to address. 

The **Rigorous Empathy Framework** reframes course design using an engineering mindset: defining systemic problems, setting measurable human-centered goals, and deploying structural syllabus policies to achieve those goals.

> [!abstract] 🎯 CENTRAL HUB: UPPER-DIVISION SURVIVAL
> ### [[Garden/Inclusive Admissions Mirage and Legacy Faculty Wall|Legacy Faculty Preparation]]
> Lower-division courses must serve as a tactical shield—training resilient students to master the professional habits, self-advocacy, and personal problem-solving flowcharts required to thrive under traditional, unyielding faculty down the road.

> [!info] 🟢 SPOKE 1: EQUITY & BACKGROUND
> * [[Garden/Inequitable Math Pipeline|The Inequitable Math Pipeline]] — Supporting varying entry-math confidence through effort-based options and forward-looking GA sessions.
> * [[Garden/Weight of First-Gen and Immigrant Expectations|First-Gen & Immigrant Expectations]] — Demystifying the "hidden curriculum" with active learning, transparent grade weighting, and predictable workloads.
> * [[Garden/Evaluation Anxiety and Systemic Failure Paralysis|Evaluation Anxiety & Failure Paralysis]] — Eliminating grade paralysis using retroactive exam replacements, exam corrections, and self-auditing Canvas tools.

> [!note] 🔵 SPOKE 2: WORK & LIFE INTEGRATION
> * [[Garden/Breadwinner-Student Disconnect|The "Breadwinner-Student" Disconnect]] — Balancing 30-hour work shifts with a 7-day rolling grace period and automated drops.
> * [[Garden/Veteran and Non-Traditional Misalignment|Veteran & Non-Traditional Misalignment]] — Respecting adult autonomy with advance materials, group problem-solving, and predictable calendars.
> * [[Garden/Institutional Convenience vs Student Growth|Institutional Boundaries vs. Growth]] — Reframing firm boundaries and 7-day hard locks as professional development rather than instructor convenience.

> [!tip] 🟣 SPOKE 3: ENGINEERING IDENTITY & MINDSET
> * [[Garden/Prerequisite Desert and Bait-and-Switch|The Prerequisite "Desert"]] — Linking calculus directly to physical engineering trade-offs via 1-page technical memos.
> * [[Garden/Mirage of Hands-On Engineering|The Mirage of "Hands-On" Engineering]] — Reframing engineering from physical tinkering to paper-based mathematical optimization and redline auditing.
