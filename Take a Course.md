## 📐 Calculus Sequence
I teach the core Engineering Calculus sequence, exclusively to students from the College of Engineering:
*   [[Courses/Calc-1/index|MATH 181: Calculus I for Engineers]] — Focuses on rates of change, limits, and derivative applications.
*   [[Courses/Calc-2/index|MATH 182: Calculus II for Engineers]] — Focuses on integration, numerical methods, and sequences/series.
*   [[Courses/Calc-3/index|MATH 283: Calculus III for Engineers]] — Focuses on multivariable calculus, vectors, and vector fields.

> [!success] How to Take my Calculus Courses
> 
> My courses are hidden from view in MyUNLV and restricted so that only engineering students may register. In order to take a course, you need to go through a few steps:
> 
> 1. **Complete a Request Form:** Receive individual permission to add the course by completing a form:
> 	1. (Coming Soon, please be patient)
> 	2. *Note: completing the form does not register you for the course, it only gives you permission to register.*
> 2. **Register for the course:** Use MyUNLV to add the course (after receiving permission by completing Step 1). *You will receive an email with detailed instructions after receiving permission by completing Step 1.*
>
> **Important!**
> My courses fill very quickly, often the wait list is as large as the enrollment! Make sure you have any registration holds removed from your account before your university-assigned registration date. **If you aren't able to add the course on your registration date, it is unlikely that you'll get a seat, and there will be nothing I can do to support.** Be ready to sign up on your registration date!
