## 📐 Calculus Sequence (Active Curriculum)
I teach the core Engineering Calculus sequence under the **Modeling Practices in Calculus (MPC)** framework:
*   [[Session 01 - Using Functions to Understand and Predict Systems|MATH 181: Calculus I for Engineers]] — Focuses on rates of change, limits, and derivative applications.
*   [[Courses/Calc-2/index|MATH 182: Calculus II for Engineers]] — Focuses on integration, numerical methods, and sequences/series.
*   [[Courses/Calc-3/index|MATH 281: Calculus III for Engineers]] — Focuses on multivariable calculus, vectors, and vector fields.
