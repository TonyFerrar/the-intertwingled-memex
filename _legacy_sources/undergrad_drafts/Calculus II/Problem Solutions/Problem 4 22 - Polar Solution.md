

#### **Solution:**

- **Substitute $x = r\cos\theta$ and $y = r\sin\theta$:**
    
    $$(r\cos\theta)^2 + (r\sin\theta)^2 = a^2$$
    
- **Factor out $r^2$:**
    
    $$r^2(\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta) = a^2$$
    
- **Apply the Pythagorean identity ($\cos^2\theta + \sin^2\theta = 1$):**
    
    $$r^2(1) = a^2$$
    
    $$r^2 = a^2$$
    
- **Solve for $r$:**
    
    $$r = a$$
