
## Part 1: Finding the Volume Using the Shell Method

To find the volume of the solid generated by revolving the region around the $y$-axis, we use the cylindrical shell formula:

$$V = \int_{a}^{b} 2\pi \cdot (\text{radius}) \cdot (\text{height}) \, dx$$

### Step 1: Identify the Components

- **Radius:** The horizontal distance from the axis of rotation ($y$-axis) to a vertical slice at position $x$. This is $\text{radius} = x$.
    
- **Height:** The vertical distance from the upper curve to the lower boundary ($x$-axis). This is $\text{height} = -\sqrt{x} + 2$.
    
- **Limits of Integration ($a$ to $b$):** The shaded region starts at the vertical line $x = 1$. It ends where the curve hits the $x$-axis ($y = 0$).
    
    To verify the upper limit:
    
    $$-\sqrt{x} + 2 = 0 \implies \sqrt{x} = 2 \implies x = 4$$
    
    So, our limits are from $x = 1$ to $x = 4$.
    

### Step 2: Set Up the Integral

Substitute these components into the shell formula:

$$V = \int_{1}^{4} 2\pi \cdot x \cdot (-\sqrt{x} + 2) \, dx$$

Pull the constant $2\pi$ out front and distribute the $x$ inside the integrand (remember that $x \cdot \sqrt{x} = x \cdot x^{1/2} = x^{3/2}$):

$$V = 2\pi \int_{1}^{4} (-x^{3/2} + 2x) \, dx$$

### Step 3: Integrate Term by Term

Apply the power rule ($\int x^n \, dx = \frac{x^{n+1}}{n+1}$) to each term:

- For $-x^{3/2}$:
    
    $$\int -x^{3/2} \, dx = -\frac{x^{5/2}}{5/2} = -\frac{2}{5}x^{5/2}$$
    
- For $2x$:
    
    $$\int 2x \, dx = x^2$$
    

Now combine them into the antiderivative:

$$V = 2\pi \left[ -\frac{2}{5}x^{5/2} + x^2 \right]_{1}^{4}$$

### Step 4: Evaluate the Limits

Now we calculate the values at the upper limit ($4$) and lower limit ($1$).

- **At $x = 4$:**
    
    $$-\frac{2}{5}(4)^{5/2} + (4)^2 = - \frac{2}{5}(32) + 16 = -\frac{64}{5} + \frac{80}{5} = \frac{16}{5}$$
    
- **At $x = 1$:**
    
    $$-\frac{2}{5}(1)^{5/2} + (1)^2 = -\frac{2}{5} + 1 = -\frac{2}{5} + \frac{5}{5} = \frac{3}{5}$$
    

Subtract the lower limit value from the upper limit value:

$$V = 2\pi \left( \frac{16}{5} - \frac{3}{5} \right) = 2\pi \left( \frac{13}{5} \right)$$

### Final Exact Answer:

$$V = \frac{26\pi}{5} \text{ cubic units} \quad (\approx 16.34)$$

## Part 2: What does this solid look like?

Because the region starts at $x = 1$ instead of $x = 0$, revolving it around the $y$-axis leaves an empty core right in the center.

- **The Core:** It has a perfectly cylindrical hole straight through the middle with a radius of $1$.
    
- **The Inside Wall:** The inner wall of this hole is a flat vertical cylinder of height $1$ (since $y = 1$ when $x = 1$).
    
- **The Outer Shape:** From the top of that inner wall, the solid gently slopes downward and outward in a curved fashion until it meets the flat table at a total radius of $4$.
    

**Analogy:** It looks like a **volcanic crater** with a perfectly vertical chimney hole, or a wide, heavy-bottomed **bundt cake** with a completely straight inner core and a flaring outer edge.
