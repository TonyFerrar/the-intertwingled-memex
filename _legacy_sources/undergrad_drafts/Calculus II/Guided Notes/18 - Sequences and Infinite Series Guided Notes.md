## Engineering Applications
In real-world engineering, these mathematical concepts form the foundational backbone for digital processing, structural simulations, and predictive modeling. **Recurrence relations** are used in digital signal processing and robotics—such as Kalman filtering—to calculate real-time system adjustments and filter out sensor noise based on sequential past data. Engineers rely on analyzing **limits and convergence** when running complex computer simulations like Finite Element Analysis (FEA) to guarantee that structural stress or aerodynamic calculations stabilize into realistic values rather than breaking down or diverging into mathematical errors. Lastly, **infinite series** allow for the simplification of complex physical phenomena, enabling financial engineers to use geometric series to evaluate long-term project viability, and electrical engineers to use Fourier series to break down, compress, and transmit data signals wirelessly.
