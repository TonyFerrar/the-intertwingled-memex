## Engineering Applications
In engineering, parametric curves are essential because they allow us to transition from static, split-function geometric shapes—like modeling a pulley system using separate equations for the top and bottom halves—into a unified, time-dependent system that traces dynamic movement. By utilizing **Skill 1** to eliminate the parameter, engineers can simplify incredibly complex physical paths, such as the cycloid path of a point on a rolling bicycle wheel, into manageable mathematical models. Meanwhile, **Skill 2** enables tracking the precise chronological direction of motion along these paths, and **Skill 3** uses parametric calculus to compute real-time rates of change (like determining the direction a particle would fly off a spinning machine part) and cumulative physical quantities (like calculating the area under a trajectory or distance traveled) directly in parameter space.
