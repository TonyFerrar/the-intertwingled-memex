# Session 21 + Definite Integrals 

## Engineering Application
The core calculus skills covered in these notes—managing net versus total area, applying the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus (FTOC), and executing $u$-substitution with changing boundaries—are directly foundational to key engineering disciplines. In civil and structural engineering, calculating the net area under varying load distributions allows engineers to draw Shear and Bending Moment Diagrams to prevent bridge or building collapses. Meanwhile, aerospace and automotive engineers use the FTOC to integrate acceleration data into velocity and net displacement, which is crucial for rocket telemetry and vehicle crash-testing. Finally, electrical engineers rely on trigonometric $u$-substitution and boundary scaling to compute Root Mean Square (RMS) voltage, transforming fluctuating alternating current (AC) signals into stable metrics for power grid design.
