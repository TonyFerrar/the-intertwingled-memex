# Session 10 + Related Rates 

## Engineering Application
Related Rates problems model how dynamically changing physical quantities constrain one another over time by using geometric formulas, multivariable implicit differentiation via the chain rule, and dimensional analysis to interpret directional rates of change. In real-world engineering, these calculus principles are foundational for civil and hydraulic engineers managing safe reservoir drainage rates and volumetric flow through dams. Furthermore, aerospace and mechanical engineers hardcode related rates into radar tracking and air traffic control systems to translate raw multi-angle distance returns into accurate relative vehicle velocities , while environmental and chemical engineers apply them to project the surface area expansion of hazardous fluid plumes or track geometric reactant changes inside industrial mixing reactors.
