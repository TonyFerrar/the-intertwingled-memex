# Session 22 + Limit Definition 

## Engineering Application
The transition from discrete Riemann sum approximations to continuous definite integrals forms the mathematical backbone of engineering, where complex physical systems are solved by partitioning them into small, manageable elements before taking the infinite limit. In structural and mechanical engineering, this concept powers the **Finite Element Method (FEM)** by slicing intricate objects into thousands of tiny geometric meshes to analyze internal stress and heat distribution. Similarly, **digital signal processing** treats sampled audio or wireless data snapshots as Riemann sums that are converted via continuous limits into clean frequencies, while **aerodynamics and robotics** rely on these exact limits to integrate local surface pressure into total vehicle lift and to accurately determine a robot's center of mass for precise balancing.
