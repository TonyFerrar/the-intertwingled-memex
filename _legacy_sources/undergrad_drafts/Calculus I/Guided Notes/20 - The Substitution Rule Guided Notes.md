# Session 20 + The Substitution Rule 

## Engineering Application
When engineers model complex real-world systems, the resulting mathematical formulas rarely align with basic, clean integration rules. To make these equations solvable, engineers rely on **Structural Vision** to identify composite functions—such as the time-dependent power expressions in an electrical circuit's transient response—allowing them to simplify chaotic equations into basic exponential integrals. They then use **Differential Balancing** to adjust for missing constants when mapping physical boundaries, which is essential for calculating exact fluid velocities in pipelines or aerodynamic drag on aircraft wings. Finally, when dealing with non-uniform physical forces like water pressure against a dam or asymmetrical weight distributions on a bridge, engineers utilize **Algebraic Remodeling** to shift variables and restructure radical stress equations. Ultimately, $u$-substitution acts as an engineer's coordinate transformation tool, allowing them to shift mathematical perspectives to ensure physical structures are built safely and efficiently.
