# Session 13 & 14 + Optimization 

## Engineering Application
In professional engineering, the calculus optimization workflows from the notes—such as isolating variables and evaluating critical points within physical constraints—are fundamentally used to maximize system performance and efficiency while minimizing raw material consumption and production costs. Structural and civil engineers apply these mathematical models to determine optimal component dimensions so that a design can withstand maximum physical loads under strict material weight limits. Packaging and logistics engineers utilize volume-maximization functions bounded by surface-area constraints to design storage and transport mediums that eliminate dead space and reduce manufacturing waste. Additionally, the conceptual technique of revolving geometric profiles to optimize three-dimensional space is a foundational tool for aerospace and process engineers tasked with maximizing internal capacity or streamlining aerodynamic profiles within highly restricted spatial configurations.
