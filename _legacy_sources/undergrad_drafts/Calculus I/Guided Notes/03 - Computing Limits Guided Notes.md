# Session 03 + Computing Limits

## Engineering Application: 
This entire unit covers the foundational principles of evaluating mathematical limits through three key skills: applying operational limit laws (such as the sum, product, and quotient rules) to break down complex expressions, analyzing piecewise functions by ensuring left- and right-sided limits match at boundary thresholds, and resolving indeterminate forms ($\frac{0}{0}$) using algebraic techniques like factoring, radical conjugation, or trigonometric substitution to eliminate zero-factors before applying direct substitution. In real-life and engineering contexts, these mathematical limits are essential for modeling physical boundaries, such as predicting the carrying capacity of changing populations, analyzing materials right up to their structural breaking point, calculating instantaneous rocket propulsion as fuel mass drops, and protecting sensitive microchips from sudden voltage spikes.
