# Session 15 + Curve Sketching 

## Engineering Application
The calculus principles of curve sketching—analyzing boundaries, critical points ($f'(x)=0$), and concavity transitions ($f''(x)=0$)—are vital across engineering disciplines for optimization, safety, and structural integrity. In civil engineering, inflection points dictate the precise layout of geometric transitions in roadways and railways to minimize lateral forces on vehicles, while in structural engineering, they pinpoint where internal stresses flip between tension and compression in bending beams. Electrical engineers track critical points and horizontal asymptotes to regulate transient voltage peaks and ensure signal stability in circuits, whereas aerospace engineers utilize them to locate maximum fluid velocity regions for aerodynamic wing design. Finally, chemical engineers monitor inflection points to identify the maximum rate of reaction, allowing them to optimize reactor cooling systems and prevent dangerous thermal runaways.
