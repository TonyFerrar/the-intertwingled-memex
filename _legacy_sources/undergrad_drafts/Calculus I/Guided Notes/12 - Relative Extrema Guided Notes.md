# Session 12 + Relative Extrema 

## Engineering Application
In engineering, finding absolute and relative extrema via derivatives is the mathematical foundation for **Optimization Design**. By setting a derivative to zero ($f'(x) = 0$) or identifying where it is undefined, engineers locate the critical points necessary to maximize or minimize real-world constraints. This analytical framework allows aerospace engineers to find the absolute minimum aerodynamic drag for fuel-efficient aircraft, civil engineers to calculate the optimal beam dimensions for maximum structural load-bearing capacity on closed intervals, electrical engineers to ensure maximum power transfer across circuit loads, and chemical engineers to utilize sign testing to safely pinpoint the absolute maximum product yield in chemical reactors. Ultimately, these calculus principles transform abstract curves into real-world efficiency, safety, and cost savings across all engineering fields.
