**Problem:** Find the average rate of change of $f(x) = \sin(x)$ over the interval $\left[0, \frac{\pi}{6}\right]$.

* **Step 1:** Identify your interval bounds: $x_1 = 0$ and $x_2 = \frac{\pi}{6}$.
* **Step 2:** Find the corresponding output coordinates:
  * $f(x_1) = \sin(0) = 0$
  * $f(x_2) = \sin\left(\frac{\pi}{6}\right) = 0.5$
* **Step 3:** Apply the average rate formula:
  $$\frac{\Delta y}{\Delta x} = \frac{f(x_2) - f(x_1)}{x_2 - x_1} = \frac{0.5 - 0}{\frac{\pi}{6} - 0} = \frac{0.5}{\approx 0.5236} \approx 0.9549$$
* **Answer:** The average rate of change is approximately **$0.9549$**.
