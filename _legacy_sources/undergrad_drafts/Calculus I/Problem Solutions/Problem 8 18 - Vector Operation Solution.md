### **Solution to Problem 8**

Here is the explanation of the geometric properties of the cross product based on your notes.

#### **1. Direction of the Cross Product Vector ($\vec{u} \times \vec{v}$)**

If two vectors $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$ lie completely flat on a two-dimensional tabletop (the $xy$-plane), their cross product $\vec{u} \times \vec{v}$ will point **perfectly vertical, straight up or straight down, perpendicular to the tabletop** (along the $z$-axis).

This direction is determined by the **Right-Hand Rule**:

- If you curl the fingers of your right hand from vector $\vec{u}$ to vector $\vec{v}$, your thumb points in the direction of the cross product $\vec{u} \times \vec{v}$.

#### **2. Meaning of the Symbol $\perp$**

The symbol **$\perp$** means **perpendicular** (or orthogonal).

In the context of the diagram in your notes, writing $\vec{u} \times \vec{v} = \perp$ is a shorthand way of stating that the resulting cross product vector forms a **$90^\circ$ angle** relative to _both_ of the original vectors, $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$.

- It cannot point along the tabletop because it must be perpendicular to every vector lying in that plane simultaneously.
    

#### **Final Answer**

The vector $\vec{u} \times \vec{v}$ will point **straight up or down out of the tabletop**, and the symbol $\perp$ indicates that this new vector is **perpendicular** to the entire plane containing $\vec{u}$ and $\vec{v}$.
