### Solution to Problem 2: Opposite Vectors

**Problem Statement:** Using the vector $\vec{PQ} = 8\hat{i} + 5\hat{j}$ from the previous problem, write down the vector that represents the opposite direction, $\vec{QP}$. What is the geometric relationship between $\vec{PQ}$ and $\vec{QP}$?

#### Step 1: Understand the Rule for Opposite Vectors

According to your notes, reversing the direction of a vector flips its start and end points, which mathematically negates all of its components. The relationship is written as:

$$\vec{QP} = -\vec{PQ}$$

#### Step 2: Substitute the Components of $\vec{PQ}$

Substitute $\vec{PQ} = 8\hat{i} + 5\hat{j}$ into the rule:

$$\vec{QP} = -(8\hat{i} + 5\hat{j})$$

Distribute the negative sign to both the $\hat{i}$ and $\hat{j}$ components:

$$\vec{QP} = -8\hat{i} - 5\hat{j}$$

#### Step 3: Explain the Geometric Relationship

Geometrically, the two vectors have the following properties:

1. **Same Magnitude (Length):** Both vectors span the exact same distance between points $P$ and $Q$.
    
    $$|\vec{PQ}| = |\vec{QP}| = \sqrt{8^2 + 5^2} = \sqrt{89}$$
    
2. **Parallel Lines:** They lie on or parallel to the exact same line in space.
    
3. **Opposite Directions:** $\vec{PQ}$ points from $P$ to $Q$ (moving right and up), while $\vec{QP}$ points from $Q$ to $P$ (moving left and down).
    

#### Final Answer

The component form of the opposite vector is:

$$\vec{QP} = -8\hat{i} - 5\hat{j}$$

**Geometric Relationship:** $\vec{PQ}$ and $\vec{QP}$ have the exact same length and are parallel, but point in 180-degree opposite directions.
