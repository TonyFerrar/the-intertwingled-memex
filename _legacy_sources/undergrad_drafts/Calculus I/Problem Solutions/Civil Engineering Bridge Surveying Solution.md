During a baseline geometric survey of a suspension bridge truss, a civil engineer establishes a coordinate system. A critical steel support cable begins at an anchor foundation pier at $A(12, 5)$ meters and connects to the top of a structural tower at $B(42, 45)$ meters.

- **Task:** Determine the spatial vector $\vec{AB}$ representing the cable's path in component form.
    

**Solution:**

1. Identify points: Initial $A(12, 5)$, Terminal $B(42, 45)$.
    
2. Compute components:
    
    - $\Delta x = 42 - 12 = 30$
        
    - $\Delta y = 45 - 5 = 40$
        
3. Write in component form:
    
    $$\vec{AB} = 30\hat{i} + 40\hat{j}$$
    

- **Meaning of the Answer & Real-Life Application:** The vector component form $30\hat{i} + 40\hat{j}$ informs the engineering team that the cable spans a horizontal gap of $30$ meters and rises vertically by $40$ meters. In structural engineering, this algebraic translation is the vital first step required to perform a static load analysis. Engineers use these components to compute the exact angle of the cable and determine how the massive tension forces will be distributed horizontally across the bridge deck and vertically down into the foundation pier, preventing structural failure under heavy traffic loads.
