**Problem:** Use the piecewise graph provided below to evaluate the exact function value $f(-1)$ and the limit value $\lim_{x \to -1} f(x)$.


**Step 1: Determine the Function Value $f(-1)$**
Look precisely along the vertical grid line $x = -1$. Ignore the trend of the lines and locate the single coordinates. 
* There is an open circle ($\circ$) at a height of $2$, meaning the curve breaks there.
* There is a solid dot ($\bullet$) down at a height of $1$.
* **Result:** $f(-1) = 1$

**Step 2: Determine the Limit $\lim_{x \to -1} f(x)$**
Trace along the actual lines of the graph with your eyes as you approach $x = -1$ from both directions:
* Traveling from the left toward $x = -1$, the curve climbs up toward the hole at height $y = 2$.
* Traveling from the right toward $x = -1$, the curve moves along a flat line heading toward that same hole at height $y = 2$.
* **Result:** $\lim_{x \to -1} f(x) = 2$

**Summary Synthesis:**
Notice that $\lim_{x \to -1} f(x) \neq f(-1)$ since $2 \neq 1$. This proves that a limit depends entirely on the expected trajectory of the path, independent of the actual point plotted at the destination.
