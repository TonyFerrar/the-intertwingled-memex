### Solution to Problem 3: Vector Magnitude

**Problem Statement:** Consider the vector $\vec{a} = 2.5\hat{i} - 5\hat{j}$. Calculate the exact magnitude (length) of this vector, $|\vec{a}|$. Round your final answer to two decimal places.

#### Step 1: Understand the Magnitude Formula

The magnitude of a two-dimensional vector $\vec{v} = v_x\hat{i} + v_y\hat{j}$ is its geometric length. Because the $x$ and $y$ components are perpendicular, they form a right triangle where the vector itself is the hypotenuse.

According to the Pythagorean theorem, the formula for the magnitude is:

$$|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{(a_x)^2 + (a_y)^2}$$

#### Step 2: Identify the Components

From the given vector $\vec{a} = 2.5\hat{i} - 5\hat{j}$:

- The horizontal component is $a_x = 2.5$
    
- The vertical component is $a_y = -5$
    

#### Step 3: Substitute and Calculate

Substitute these components into the formula. Note that squaring a negative number always results in a positive number:

$$|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{(2.5)^2 + (-5)^2}$$

$$|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{6.25 + 25}$$

$$|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{31.25}$$

#### Step 4: Approximate to Two Decimal Places

Using a calculator to find the square root of $31.25$:

$$|\vec{a}| \approx 5.590169...$$

Rounding to two decimal places gives **5.59**.

#### Final Answer

The magnitude of the vector is:

$$|\vec{a}| = \sqrt{31.25} \approx 5.59$$
