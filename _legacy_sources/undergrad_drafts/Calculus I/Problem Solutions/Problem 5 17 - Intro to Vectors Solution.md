### Solution to Problem 5: Vector Subtraction

**Problem Statement:** Using the same two vectors from Problem 4:

$\vec{u} = 3\hat{i} - 2\hat{j}$

$\vec{v} = 1\hat{i} + 4\hat{j}$

Compute the vector subtraction $\vec{v} - \vec{u}$. Be mindful of the order and the negative signs.

#### Step 1: Understand the Vector Subtraction Rule

To subtract one vector from another, you subtract their corresponding components. It is critical to follow the requested order ($\vec{v} - \vec{u}$ means components of $\vec{u}$ are subtracted _from_ components of $\vec{v}$):

$$\vec{v} - \vec{u} = (v_x - u_x)\hat{i} + (v_y - u_y)\hat{j}$$

Geometrically, subtracting $\vec{u}$ from $\vec{v}$ is equivalent to adding the opposite of $\vec{u}$ to $\vec{v}$ (i.e., $\vec{v} + (-\vec{u})$).

#### Step 2: Identify the Components

From the given vectors:

- For $\vec{v}$: $v_x = 1$ and $v_y = 4$
    
- For $\vec{u}$: $u_x = 3$ and $u_y = -2$
    

#### Step 3: Substitute and Calculate

Group the components carefully, paying close attention to the double negative in the $y$-component:

$$\vec{v} - \vec{u} = (1 - 3)\hat{i} + (4 - (-2))\hat{j}$$

Simplify both parts:

- For $\hat{i}$: $1 - 3 = -2$
    
- For $\hat{j}$: $4 - (-2) = 4 + 2 = 6$
    

$$\vec{v} - \vec{u} = -2\hat{i} + 6\hat{j}$$

#### Final Answer

The resulting vector of the subtraction is:

$$\vec{v} - \vec{u} = -2\hat{i} + 6\hat{j}$$
