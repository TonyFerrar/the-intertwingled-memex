### **Solution to Problem 6**

Here is the step-by-step solution for calculating the algebraic dot product of two vectors.

#### **Formula**

The dot product of two 2D vectors $\vec{u} = \langle u_x, u_y \rangle$ and $\vec{v} = \langle v_x, v_y \rangle$ is calculated by multiplying corresponding components together and adding the results:

$$\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = u_x v_x + u_y v_y$$

> **Note:** The dot product always results in a single scalar number, never another vector.

#### **Given Vectors**

$$\vec{u} = \langle 2, -3 \rangle$$

$$\vec{v} = \langle 4, 5 \rangle$$

- $u_x = 2$, $u_y = -3$
    
- $v_x = 4$, $v_y = 5$
    

#### **Step-by-Step Calculation**

1. **Multiply the horizontal ($x$) components together:**
    
    $$u_x v_x = (2)(4) = 8$$
    
2. **Multiply the vertical ($y$) components together:**
    
    $$u_y v_y = (-3)(5) = -15$$
    
3. Add the two products together:
    
    $$\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 8 + (-15)$$
    
    $$\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = 8 - 15$$
    
    $$\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v} = -7$$
    

#### **Final Answer**

The dot product $\vec{u} \cdot \vec{v}$ is **$-7$**.
