**Problem 5: Linearization Near a Perfect Square Point**
Instead of evaluating non-linear operations exactly, we can construct a tangent line approximation $T(x)$ near a known baseline point $x_0$. 
1. Find the linear approximation $T(x)$ for the function $f(x) = \sqrt{x}$ centered at the easy-to-calculate point $x_0 = 9$.
2. Use your formula for $T(x)$ to estimate the value of $\sqrt{9.3}$ without a calculator.
