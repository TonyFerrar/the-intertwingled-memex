$$S(x) = \begin{cases} 8x & : 0 \le x < 10 \\ 0 & : x \ge 10 \quad \text{(The wire snaps completely)} \end{cases}$$

Analyze the system boundaries around the failure point by evaluating:
1. $\lim_{x\to10^{-}} S(x)$
2. $\lim_{x\to10^{+}} S(x)$
