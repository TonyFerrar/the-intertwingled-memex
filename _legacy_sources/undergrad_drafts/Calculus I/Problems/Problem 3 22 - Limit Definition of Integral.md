Setting Up the Limit Definition

Consider the definite integral:

$$\int_{1}^{4} x^3 \, dx$$

Write out the formal expression for this integral as a limit of a Right Riemann Sum.

- Find $\Delta x$ in terms of $n$.
    
- Find the right-endpoint expression for $x_i$ in terms of $i$ and $n$.
    
- Set up—but do not evaluate—the complete limit expression: $\lim_{n \to \infty} \sum_{i=1}^n f(x_i)\Delta x$.
