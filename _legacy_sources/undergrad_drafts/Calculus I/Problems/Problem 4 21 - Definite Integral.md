Evaluate the following definite integrals. If substitution is used, be sure to clearly indicate $u$ and $du$.

(d) $\int_{0}^{\pi/4}\tan(\theta)\sec^{2}(\theta)d\theta$  

(e) $\int_{-2}^{-1}x\sqrt[4]{x+2}dx$  

(f) $\int_{1}^{3}\frac{e^{1/x}}{x^{2}}dx$
