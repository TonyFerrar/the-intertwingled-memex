Approximating Area with Right Riemann Sums

Let $f(x) = x^2$ on the interval $[0, 2]$.

1. Determine the width of each subinterval, $\Delta x$, if the interval is split into $n = 4$ equal parts.
    
2. Find the right-endpoint $x$-values ($x_1, x_2, x_3, x_4$) for each subinterval.
    
3. Estimate the total area under the curve by calculating the Right Riemann Sum.
