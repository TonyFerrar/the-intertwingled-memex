A civil engineer is inspecting a support cable on a suspension bridge truss. The force vector being exerted by the cable on an anchor pin is recorded by sensors as $\vec{F} = 15\hat{i} - 36\hat{j}$ kilonewtons (kN). The structural safety manual states that this specific anchor pin will sheer and fail if it experiences a total absolute force exceeding $40$ kN.

- **Task:** Calculate the total absolute magnitude of the force vector, and determine if the anchor pin is safe.
