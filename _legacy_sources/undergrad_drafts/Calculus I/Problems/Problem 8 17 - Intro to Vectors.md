Vector Trigonometry and Direction Angle

A vector $\vec{V}$ has an $x$-component of $V_x = 3$ and a $y$-component of $V_y = -2$.

- **Task:** Write out the trigonometric formula involving the inverse tangent ($\tan^{-1}$) used to find the direction angle $\theta$, and set up the calculation to find the angle relative to the positive x-axis.
