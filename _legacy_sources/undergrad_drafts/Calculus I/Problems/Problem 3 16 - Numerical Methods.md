**Problem 3: Deriving the Iteration Formula**
The Newton-Raphson method approximates roots of a non-linear equation $f(x) = 0$ by iteratively tracking the $x$-intercepts of local tangent lines. Given the function:
$$f(x) = 2x^3 - 6x + 1$$
1. Find the analytical derivative $f'(x)$.
2. Use the general Newton-Raphson recurrence relation $x_{n+1} = x_n - \frac{f(x_n)}{f'(x_n)}$ to write the explicit algebraic expression for $x_{n+1}$ in terms of $x_n$.
