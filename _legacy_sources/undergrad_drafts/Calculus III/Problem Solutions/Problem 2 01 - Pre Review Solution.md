Here is the step-by-step solution to find the length of side $a$ from Question 2.

### Step 1: Identify the Given Information

From the problem description and the right triangle diagram, we have:

- **Angle $A$:** $63^\circ$
    
- **Hypotenuse ($c$):** $18$
    
- **Opposite side to angle $A$:** $a$
    

### Step 2: Choose the Correct Trigonometric Ratio

To link the angle, the opposite side, and the hypotenuse, use the **sine** function:

$$\sin(A) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}$$

Substituting our values into the formula:

$$\sin(63^\circ) = \frac{a}{18}$$

### Step 3: Isolate the Variable $a$

Multiply both sides of the equation by 18 to solve for $a$:

$$a = 18 \cdot \sin(63^\circ)$$

### Step 4: Calculate and Round the Value

Using a calculator set to **Degree** mode:

$$\sin(63^\circ) \approx 0.8910065$$

Now, multiply by 18:

$$a \approx 18 \cdot 0.8910065 \approx 16.038117$$

Rounding to 2 decimal places as requested:

$$a \approx 16.04$$

### Final Answer

$$a \approx 16.04$$
