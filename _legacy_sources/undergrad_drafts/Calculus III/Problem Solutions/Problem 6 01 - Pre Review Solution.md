Here is the step-by-step solution to convert the angle from degrees to radians for **Question 6**.

### Step 1: Understand the Conversion Factor

To convert an angle from degrees to radians, we use the fact that a half-circle measures both $180^\circ$ and $\pi$ radians.

Therefore, the conversion formula is:

$$\text{Radians} = \text{Degrees} \times \frac{\pi}{180^\circ}$$

### Step 2: Substitute the Given Value

Substitute the given angle of $23^\circ$ into the formula:

$$\text{Radians} = 23^\circ \times \frac{\pi}{180^\circ}$$

### Step 3: Simplify the Fraction

Combine the expression into a single fraction:

$$\text{Radians} = \frac{23\pi}{180}$$

Since $23$ is a prime number and does not share any common factors with $180$, the fraction cannot be reduced any further.

### Final Answer

$$\frac{23\pi}{180} \text{ rad}$$
