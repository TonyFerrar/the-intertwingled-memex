## Solution: Problem 2

### Part 1: Evaluate $F(1,2)$
Given the 2D vector field:
$$F(x,y) = \langle 2xy, x + 3y \rangle$$

To evaluate the field at the specific point $(x, y) = (1, 2)$, substitute $x = 1$ and $y = 2$ into the component functions:

* **$P(1,2)$ component:** $2(1)(2) = 4$
* **$Q(1,2)$ component:** $1 + 3(2) = 1 + 6 = 7$

$$F(1,2) = \langle 4, 7 \rangle$$

### Part 2: Evaluate $G(1,2,3)$
Given the 3D vector field:
$$G(x,y,z) = \langle yz, xz, xy \rangle$$

To evaluate the field at the specific point $(x, y, z) = (1, 2, 3)$, substitute $x = 1$, $y = 2$, and $z = 3$ into the component functions:

* **$P(1,2,3)$ component ($yz$):** $2 \cdot 3 = 6$
* **$Q(1,2,3)$ component ($xz$):** $1 \cdot 3 = 3$
* **$R(1,2,3)$ component ($xy$):** $1 \cdot 2 = 2$

$$G(1,2,3) = \langle 6, 3, 2 \rangle$$
