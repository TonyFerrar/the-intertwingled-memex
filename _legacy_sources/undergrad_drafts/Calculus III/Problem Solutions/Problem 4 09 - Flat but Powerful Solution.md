**Problem:** Find the equation of the tangent plane to $z = \sin(xy)$ at $(x_{0}, y_{0}) = (0, \pi)$, where $z_{0} = 0$.

**Solution:**
1. Compute the partial derivatives:
   $$f_x(x,y) = y\cos(xy)$$
   $$f_y(x,y) = x\cos(xy)$$
2. Evaluate the partial derivatives at $(0, \pi)$:
   $$f_x(0, \pi) = \pi \cos(0) = \pi$$
   $$f_y(0, \pi) = 0 \cos(0) = 0$$
3. Substitute into the tangent plane formula:
   $$z = 0 + \pi(x - 0) + 0(y - \pi)$$
   $$z = \pi x$$
