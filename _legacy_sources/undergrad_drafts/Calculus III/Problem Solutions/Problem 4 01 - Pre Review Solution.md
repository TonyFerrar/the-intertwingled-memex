Here is the step-by-step solution to find the exact values of $\sin A$ and $\cos A$ from Question 4.

### Step 1: Identify the Given Information

From the right triangle diagram and details, we have:

- **Adjacent side ($b$):** $6$
    
- **Opposite side ($a$):** $6$
    
- **Hypotenuse ($c$):** $c$
    

### Step 2: Find the Hypotenuse ($c$)

Before finding the sine and cosine ratios, we need the length of the hypotenuse. Use the **Pythagorean theorem**:

$$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$$

Substituting the known values:

$$6^2 + 6^2 = c^2$$

$$36 + 36 = c^2$$

$$72 = c^2$$

Take the square root of both sides to find $c$:

$$c = \sqrt{72}$$

Simplify the radical by pulling out the largest perfect square factor ($36$):

$$c = \sqrt{36 \cdot 2} = 6\sqrt{2}$$

### Step 3: Evaluate $\sin A$

The sine ratio is defined as:

$$\sin A = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}$$

Substituting our side lengths:

$$\sin A = \frac{6}{6\sqrt{2}}$$

Cancel out the common factor of 6:

$$\sin A = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$$

Rationalize the denominator by multiplying the top and bottom by $\sqrt{2}$:

$$\sin A = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$$

### Step 4: Evaluate $\cos A$

The cosine ratio is defined as:

$$\cos A = \frac{\text{Adjacent}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}$$

Substituting our side lengths:

$$\cos A = \frac{6}{6\sqrt{2}}$$

Cancel out the common factor of 6:

$$\cos A = \frac{1}{\sqrt{2}}$$

Rationalize the denominator:

$$\cos A = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$$

### Final Answer

$$\sin A = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$$

$$\cos A = \frac{\sqrt{2}}{2}$$
