Here is the step-by-step solution to find the exact length of side $a$ from Question 3.

### Step 1: Identify the Given Information

From the right triangle diagram and details, we have:

- **Hypotenuse ($c$):** $12$
    
- **Adjacent side ($b$):** $9$
    
- **Opposite side ($a$):** $a$
    

### Step 2: Choose the Correct Formula

Since this is a right triangle and we are given two side lengths, use the **Pythagorean theorem**:

$$a^2 + b^2 = c^2$$

Substituting the known values into the equation:

$$a^2 + 9^2 = 12^2$$

### Step 3: Simplify and Isolate $a^2$

Calculate the squares of the numbers:

$$a^2 + 81 = 144$$

Subtract 81 from both sides to isolate $a^2$:

$$a^2 = 144 - 81$$

$$a^2 = 63$$

### Step 4: Solve for $a$ and Simplify to Exact Form

Take the principal square root of both sides (since a length must be positive):

$$a = \sqrt{63}$$

Simplify the radical by finding the largest perfect square factor of 63, which is 9:

$$a = \sqrt{9 \cdot 7}$$

$$a = 3\sqrt{7}$$

### Final Answer

$$a = 3\sqrt{7}$$
