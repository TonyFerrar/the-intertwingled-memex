Here is the step-by-step solution to convert the angle from radians to degrees for **Question 5**.

### Step 1: Understand the Conversion Factor

To convert an angle from radians to degrees, we use the relationship that a straight line (half a circle) is equal to both $\pi$ radians and $180^\circ$.

Therefore, the conversion formula is:

$$\text{Degrees} = \text{Radians} \times \frac{180^\circ}{\pi}$$

### Step 2: Substitute the Given Value

Substitute the given value of $\frac{7\pi}{12}$ rad into our formula:

$$\text{Degrees} = \left(\frac{7\pi}{12}\right) \times \frac{180^\circ}{\pi}$$

### Step 3: Simplify the Expression

First, cancel out the $\pi$ in the numerator and the denominator:

$$\text{Degrees} = \frac{7}{12} \times 180^\circ$$

Next, simplify the fraction by dividing $180^\circ$ by $12$:

$$\frac{180^\circ}{12} = 15^\circ$$

Now, multiply the remaining numbers:

$$\text{Degrees} = 7 \times 15^\circ = 105^\circ$$

### Final Answer

$$105^\circ$$
