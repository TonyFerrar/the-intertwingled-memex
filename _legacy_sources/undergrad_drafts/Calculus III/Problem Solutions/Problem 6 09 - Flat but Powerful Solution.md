**Problem:** Find the equation of the tangent plane to $z = \sqrt{x+y}$ at $(3, 1)$, where $z = 2$.

**Solution:**
1. Compute the partial derivatives:
   $$f_x(x,y) = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x+y}}$$
   $$f_y(x,y) = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{x+y}}$$
2. Evaluate at $(3, 1)$:
   $$f_x(3, 1) = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{3+1}} = \frac{1}{4}$$
   $$f_y(3, 1) = \frac{1}{2\sqrt{3+1}} = \frac{1}{4}$$
3. Substitute into the formula:
   $$z = 2 + \frac{1}{4}(x - 3) + \frac{1}{4}(y - 1)$$
   $$z = 2 + \frac{1}{4}x - \frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{4}y - \frac{1}{4}$$
   $$z = \frac{1}{4}x + \frac{1}{4}y + 1$$
