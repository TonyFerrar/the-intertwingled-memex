Here is the step-by-step solution for **Question 7**.

### Step 1: Visualize the Scenario as a Right Triangle

The situation describes a right triangle where:

- The vertical height of the staircase is the side **opposite** to the angle: $\text{Opposite} = 6\text{ ft}$.
    
- The angle between the ground and the ramp is the angle of elevation: $\theta = 26^\circ$.
    
- The length of the ramp is the **hypotenuse** ($x$) of the triangle because it spans from the ground to the top of the stairs.
    

### Step 2: Select the Correct Trigonometric Ratio

Since we are given the **opposite** side and need to find the **hypotenuse**, we use the **sine** function:

$$\sin(\theta) = \frac{\text{Opposite}}{\text{Hypotenuse}}$$

Substitute the given values into the equation:

$$\sin(26^\circ) = \frac{6}{x}$$

### Step 3: Isolate the Variable $x$

Multiply both sides by $x$ to clear the fraction:

$$x \cdot \sin(26^\circ) = 6$$

Now, divide both sides by $\sin(26^\circ)$ to solve for $x$:

$$x = \frac{6}{\sin(26^\circ)}$$

### Step 4: Calculate and Round the Answer

Using a calculator set to **Degree** mode, find the value of $\sin(26^\circ)$:

$$\sin(26^\circ) \approx 0.438371$$

Now, divide 6 by that value:

$$x \approx \frac{6}{0.438371} \approx 13.687032$$

Rounding to four decimal places as instructed in the problem:

$$x \approx 13.6870\text{ ft}$$

### Final Answer

$$13.6870\text{ ft}$$
