**Problem:** Find the equation of the tangent plane to $z = x^{2} + y^{2}$ at $(1, 2)$, where $z = 5$.

**Solution:**
1. Compute the partial derivatives:
   $$f_x(x,y) = 2x$$
   $$f_y(x,y) = 2y$$
2. Evaluate at $(1, 2)$:
   $$f_x(1, 2) = 2(1) = 2$$
   $$f_y(1, 2) = 2(2) = 4$$
3. Substitute into the formula:
   $$z = 5 + 2(x - 1) + 4(y - 2)$$
   $$z = 5 + 2x - 2 + 4y - 8$$
   $$z = 2x + 4y - 5$$
