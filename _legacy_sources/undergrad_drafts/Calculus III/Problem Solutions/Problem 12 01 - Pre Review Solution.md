- $f(x)$ is **concave up** when $f''(x) > 0$.

- A positive slope ($f''(x) > 0$) means the graph of $f'(x)$ must be **increasing** (moving upwards from left to right).
    

Therefore, finding where $f(x)$ is concave up is the exact same task as finding the intervals where the graph of $f'(x)$ goes upward.
