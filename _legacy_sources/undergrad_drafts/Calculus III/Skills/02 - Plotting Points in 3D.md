# Skill 1: Plotting Points in 3D

## Essential Notes & Concepts

To navigate a three-dimensional world, we extend the traditional 2D Cartesian plane by adding a third axis: the **z-axis**. This allows us to locate any object in space using a unique triplet of coordinates.

### The 3D Coordinate System

- **Coordinates:** A point $P$ in 3D space is written as an ordered triplet: $P = (x, y, z)$.
    
- **Axes:** The space is defined by three mutually perpendicular lines intersecting at a single point called the **origin $(0,0,0)$**.
    
- **The Right-Hand Rule:** This rule standardizes the orientation of the axes. If you point the index finger of your right hand along the positive x-axis and curl your middle finger toward the positive y-axis, your thumb will point directly along the positive z-axis.
    

### Distance Formula in 3D Space

The straight-line distance $d$ between any two points in space, $P_1 = (x_1, y_1, z_1)$ and $P_2 = (x_2, y_2, z_2)$, is a simple extension of the Pythagorean theorem:

$$d = \sqrt{(x_2 - x_1)^2 + (y_2 - y_1)^2 + (z_2 - z_1)^2}$$

## Sample Problems (Guided Notes Practice)

### Demo Problem 1.1

**Problem:** Establish axes in the correct orientation according to the right hand rule and plot the 3 given points:

1. $P_1 = (2, 4, 3)$
    
2. $P_2 = (0, 5, 2)$
    
3. $P_3 = (-1, -2, 3)$
    

**Solution & Graphing Strategy:**

1. **Set Up the Axis System:** In a standard isometric 3D drawing grid, point the positive x-axis down and to the left, the positive y-axis horizontally to the right, and the positive z-axis straight up.
    
2. **Plotting $P_1(2, 4, 3)$:** Start at $(0,0,0)$. Move forward 2 units on the x-axis, move right 4 units parallel to the y-axis, and move up 3 units parallel to the z-axis.
    
3. **Plotting $P_2(0, 5, 2)$:** Start at $(0,0,0)$. Move 0 units along the x-axis. Move right 5 units along the y-axis, then move up 2 units parallel to the z-axis.
    
4. **Plotting $P_3(-1, -2, 3)$:** Start at $(0,0,0)$. Move backward 1 unit along the negative x-axis (up-right direction). Move left 2 units parallel to the negative y-axis. Move up 3 units parallel to the z-axis.
