## Engineering Applications 
In engineering, these three skills serve as the foundational tools used to simplify complex, non-linear physical systems into manageable, linear mathematics. Engineers use the equations of planes to define structural boundaries and coordinate systems in CAD software and robotic kinematics. Tangent planes allow aerospace and mechanical engineers to analyze curved surfaces—such as airplane wings or car bodies—by breaking them down into local, flat surfaces where fluid dynamics, aerodynamic drag, and directional stresses can be calculated using normal vectors. Finally, linear approximations act as the ultimate real-time computational shortcut; by substituting a highly complex, curved function with its flat tangent plane, onboard computers in autonomous drones, self-driving cars, and finite element analysis (FEA) software can instantly estimate system behaviors and make rapid control adjustments without overwhelming their processors.
