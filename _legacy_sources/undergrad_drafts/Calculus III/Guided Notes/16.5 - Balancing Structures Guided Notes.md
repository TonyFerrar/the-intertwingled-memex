## Engineering Applications
In real-world engineering, these three skills allow designers to model how material is distributed to ensure physical balance, structural efficiency, and rotational stability before a prototype is ever built. First, **2D center of mass calculations** locate precise balance points for camera gimbals, robotic arms, and sheet metal brackets to prevent motor burnout and unwanted bending leverage. Second, **2D moments of inertia and radii of gyration** dictate the bending resistance of structural I-beams and wind turbine blades, as well as the torque required to operate automotive brake disks and clutch plates. Finally, **3D center of mass and rotational inertia calculations** apply triple integrals across cylindrical and spherical systems to stabilize rocket flight trajectories, eliminate destructive wobbling in high-speed jet turbines and flywheels, and ensure massive architectural domes and support pillars resist tipping under environmental loads.
