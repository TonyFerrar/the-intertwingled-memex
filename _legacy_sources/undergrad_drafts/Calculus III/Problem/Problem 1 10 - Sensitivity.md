A thin metal plate has a temperature field $T(x, y) = 5x^2y + 2y^3$, where $x$ and $y$ are coordinates in centimeters. A sensor moves across the plate following the path $x(t) = \sin(2t)$ and $y(t) = e^t$, where $t$ is time in seconds.

Find the rate at which the sensor's measured temperature is changing, $\frac{dT}{dt}$, when $t = 0$.
