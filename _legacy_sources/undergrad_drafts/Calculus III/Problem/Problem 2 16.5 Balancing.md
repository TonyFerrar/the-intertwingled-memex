Linear Density Gradient on a Triangle 

**Scenario:** A bracket is manufactured such that the metal cools unevenly, causing a linear density shift from one side to the other.

- **The Problem:** A thin triangular plate is bounded by $y = 0$, $x = 2$, and $y = 3x$. Its density varies across its width such that:
    
    $$\rho(x, y) = 2x$$
    
    Calculate the total mass $M$ of the lamina and find its center of mass coordinates $(\overline{x}, \overline{y})$.
    
- **What this tests:** Setting up non-constant limits of integration for a boundary line ($y = 3x$) and integrating a variable density function $\rho(x,y)$.
