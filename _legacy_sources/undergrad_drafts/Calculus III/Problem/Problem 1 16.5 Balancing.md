The Uniform Rectangular Centroid (Level: Easiest)

**Scenario:** An engineer is checking the baseline balance point of a simple rectangular structural plate.

- **The Problem:** Find the centroid $(\overline{x}, \overline{y})$ of a uniform lamina bounded by $x = 0$, $x = 4$, $y = 0$, and $y = 2$ with a constant density $\rho = 3$.
    
- **What this tests:** Basic mechanics of the mass and center of mass formulas for 2D setups.
    
- **Hint:** Since the density $\rho$ is constant, it will cancel out of your coordinate equations, meaning the center of mass is purely geometric (the centroid). You can double-check your calculus using basic intuition here!
