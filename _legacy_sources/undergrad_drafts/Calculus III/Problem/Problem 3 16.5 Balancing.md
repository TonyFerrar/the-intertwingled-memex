Rotational Inertia of a Structural Sheet (Level: Intermediate-Easy)

**Scenario:** You need to determine how much torque is required to rotate a rectangular component in a mechanical assembly.

- **The Problem:** A rectangular lamina occupies the region bounded by $0 \le x \le 3$ and $0 \le y \le 2$ with a uniform density $\rho = 1$. Compute the second moments of inertia $I_x$ and $I_y$, and use them to find the radius of gyration $k_z$ about the origin.
    
- **What this tests:** Applying the second-moment formulas ($I_x$ uses $y^2$ and $I_y$ uses $x^2$) and calculating the effective distance of mass distribution ($k = \sqrt{I/M}$).
    
- **Pitfall Warning:** Keep your variables straight! Don't accidentally swap the squared distance terms when shifting between $I_x$ and $I_y$.
