#### Scenario:

An energy storage engineer is optimizing a cylindrical flywheel rotor designed to store kinetic energy in an industrial power grid. The rotor is bounded by radius $R = 2 \text{ dm}$ and height $h = 4 \text{ dm}$ ($0 \le z \le 4 \text{ dm}$).

Due to centrifugal casting, the composite alloy density increases radially outward from the spin axis:

$$\rho(r) = 1 + r^2 \quad (\text{in kg/dm}^3)$$

1. Compute total mass $M$, rotational inertia $I_z$, and polar radius of gyration $k_z$.
    
2. Determine kinetic energy stored $E_k$ when spinning at $\omega = 300 \text{ rad/s}$ ($E_k = \frac{1}{2} I_z \omega^2$).
