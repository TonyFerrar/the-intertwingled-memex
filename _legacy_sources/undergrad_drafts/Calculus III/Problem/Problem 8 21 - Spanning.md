Suppose a wall in the plane $z = 0$ has a heat flux vector field given below:
$$\vec{F}(x,y,z) = \langle 0, 0, -50 \rangle \text{ W/m}^2$$

The negative $z$-direction means the heat is flowing from the room (positive side) towards the outside (negative side).

Consider the rectangular cross section of the wall:
$$0 \le x \le 4, \quad 0 \le y \le 3, \quad z = 0$$

Take the outward normal to be $\vec{n} = \langle 0, 0, -1 \rangle$. Compute the total heat flow rate (W) through this wall section.
