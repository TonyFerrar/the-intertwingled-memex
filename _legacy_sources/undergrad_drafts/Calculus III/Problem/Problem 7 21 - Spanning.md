A fluid flows in the positive $z$ direction with the velocity field:
$$\vec{v}(x,y,z) = \langle 0, 0, 3 \rangle \text{ m/s}$$

The fluid passes through a circular opening in the plane $z = 0$ given by $x^2 + y^2 \le 1$ with the surface oriented in the direction of the flow ($+z$).

Compute the volumetric flow rate $Q$ (How much volume of water is flowing through the surface per second, $\text{m}^3/\text{s}$) through the opening.
