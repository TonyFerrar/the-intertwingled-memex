An electrical engineer maps out the local electric field $\mathbf{E}$ (in Newtons per Coulomb) surrounding a specialized high-voltage power transmission component. The field vector map is recorded as:

$$\mathbf{E}(x,y,z) = (x^2)\mathbf{i} + (y^2)\mathbf{j} + (z^2)\mathbf{k}$$

Evaluate $\nabla \cdot \mathbf{E}$ and $\nabla \times \mathbf{E}$ at the coordinate point $(1, 1, 1)$, and translate what these findings reveal about the local electrical charges.
