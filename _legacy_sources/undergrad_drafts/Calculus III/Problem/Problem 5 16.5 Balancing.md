The Uniform 3D Wedge (Level: Intermediate-Hard)

**Scenario:** A concrete support pillar is cast in the shape of a simple 3D tetrahedral wedge.

- **The Problem:** A solid 3D structural component is bounded by the coordinate planes ($x = 0, y = 0, z = 0$) and the sloped plane $x + y + z = 2$. Assuming a uniform material density $\rho = 1$, set up and evaluate the triple integrals to determine its total mass $M$ and its center of mass $(\overline{x}, \overline{y}, \overline{z})$.
    
- **What this tests:** Transitioning to Skill 3 (3D space), setting up changing 3D integration limits, and managing three first-moment integrals ($M_{yz}, M_{xz}, M_{xy}$).
