Consider the portion of the plane $x + 2y + 3z = 6$ lying above the rectangle characterized below:
$$0 \le x \le 2, \quad 0 \le y \le 1$$

1. Give the parametrization $\vec{r}(x,y)$ of the surface using $(x,y)$ as parameters.
2. Compute partial derivatives and find the normal vector $\vec{r}_x \times \vec{r}_y$.
3. Solve for $dS$ in terms of $dx \, dy$.
