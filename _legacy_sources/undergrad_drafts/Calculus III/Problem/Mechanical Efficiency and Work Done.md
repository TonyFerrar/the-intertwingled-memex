**Problem Statement:** A mechanical conveyor system uses an electric motor to pull a heavy cargo crate along a flat, horizontal tracking rail. The displacement vector of the crate is $\vec{d} = 12\hat{i}\text{ meters}$. Due to the structural positioning of the mechanical pulley, the cable pulls on the crate with a constant force vector of $\vec{F} = 350\hat{i} + 120\hat{j}\text{ Newtons}$. Compute the work done on the crate using the dot product.
