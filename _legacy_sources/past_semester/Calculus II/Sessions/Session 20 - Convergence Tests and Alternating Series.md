# Session 20 - Convergence Tests and Alternating Series

## Session Overview
This session provides a comprehensive overview of **convergence and divergence tests** for infinite series. You'll learn the essential methods for analyzing series with **positive terms**, such as the **integral, comparison, and divergence tests**, alongside specialized tools like the **ratio and root tests**. This session specifically addresses **alternating series**, detailing the criteria for **absolute and conditional convergence** through the **Alternating Series Test**. Complex concepts are reinforced with step-by-step examples involving **p-series** and the **alternating harmonic series**.

## Skills You'll Learn
1. [[Applying tests to determine the convergence or divergence of positive-term series]]
2. [[Analyzing alternating series to determine absolute versus conditional convergence]]
3. [[Utilizing the Ratio and Root Tests for complex series]]
