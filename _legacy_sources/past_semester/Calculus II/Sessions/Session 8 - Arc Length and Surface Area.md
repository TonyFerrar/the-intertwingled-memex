# Session 8 - Arc Length and Surface Area

## Session Overview
This session provides a comprehensive guide to calculating **arc length** and **surface area** using definite integrals. **Arc length** is derived by summing infinitely small straight-line segments along a curve, a process formalized through the **Mean Value Theorem** and Riemann sums. Building on this concept, the **surface area** of a solid of revolution is found by calculating the area of infinitesimal **cone frustums** generated as a curve rotates around an axis. We will explore specific **mathematical theorems** and formulas for functions defined by either $x$ or $y$ variables.
