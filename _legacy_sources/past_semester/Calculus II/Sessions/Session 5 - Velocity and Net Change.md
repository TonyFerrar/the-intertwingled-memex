# Session 5 - Velocity and Net Change

## Session Overview
This session is all about how engineers use **integrals** to calculate **displacement**, **total distance**, and **net change**. We will see the definition of **velocity** as the derivative of **position** and how to integrate velocity to yield position. These calculus concepts apply to any quantity's **future value**. When engineers predict the future, the concepts of integrals and net change immediately arise.
