# Session 23 - Parametric Curves

## Session Overview
This session introduces the concept of **parametric curves**, which define coordinate points using an independent third variable known as a **parameter**. By representing $x$ and $y$ as separate functions of $t$, we can more easily model complex shapes like **cycloids** and determine a curve's **orientation**. We'll explore the details of specific techniques for **calculating derivatives** and finding the **area under a curve** directly from these equations without needing to convert them back into standard rectangular forms. Through various examples and theorems, we will demonstrate how **calculus principles** are adapted to analyze motion and geometric figures in these alternative coordinate systems.
