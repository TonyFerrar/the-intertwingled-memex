# Session 19 - Sequences and Infinite Series

## Session Overview
These session provides a foundational introduction to **sequences and series** within the context of **Calculus II**. A **sequence** is defined as an ordered collection of numbers that can be generated using either **recursive rules** or **explicit formulas**. If the terms of a sequence approach a specific value as they progress toward infinity, the sequence **converges**; otherwise, it **diverges**. Furthermore, the session explores **infinite series**, which represent the total sum of all terms in a sequence. Special attention is given to **geometric series**, where convergence depends on the ratio between terms, and **telescoping series**, which simplify through internal cancellation. Ultimately, a series only possesses a finite sum if its **sequence of partial sums** approaches a specific limit.
