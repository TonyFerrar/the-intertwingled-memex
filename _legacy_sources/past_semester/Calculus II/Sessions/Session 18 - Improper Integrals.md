# Session 18 - Improper Integrals

## Session Overview
This session provides an overview of **improper integrals**, which are definite integrals that involve either an **infinite domain** or a **discontinuous integrand**. To evaluate these expressions, the text explains how to utilize **limits** to determine if the area under a curve **converges** to a finite value or **diverges** toward infinity. Special attention is given to **p-integrals**, offering specific rules for their behavior based on the value of the exponent. Furthermore, this session introduces the **Comparison Test**, a strategy used to judge the convergence of a complex function by relating it to a simpler, known integral. Through various examples and rules, this experience establishes a mathematical framework for solving problems where traditional **bounded integration** methods fail.
