
# Session 3 - Approximating Definite Integrals with Riemann Sums

## Session Overview
In this session, you'll learn how to calculate the **[[accumulated change]]** of a quantity, such as distance, by determining the **area under a curve** on a graph. While constant rates of change create simple rectangles, fluctuating rates require more complex **[[approximation techniques]]** known as **[[Riemann sums]]**. These methods involve dividing the area into multiple vertical rectangles and calculating their total size using **left-hand**, **right-hand**, or **midpoint** evaluation points. An important observation: as the number of rectangles increases, the estimate becomes more precise. Ultimately, the **[[definite integral]]** arises as the mathematical limit of this process, representing the exact area when the number of subintervals reaches infinity.
