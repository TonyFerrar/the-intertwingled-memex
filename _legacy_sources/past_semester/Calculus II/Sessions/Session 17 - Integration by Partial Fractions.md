# Session 17 - Integration by Partial Fractions

## Session Overview
This session prepares you for performing **integration by partial fractions** on complex rational functions. This integration technique undoes the quotient rule of derivatives. 

The process involves simplifying difficult integrals by breaking them down into a **sum of smaller fractions**, a process known as **partial fraction decomposition**. There are slightly different strategies based on whether the denominator contains **simple linear factors**, **repeated linear factors**, or **irreducible quadratic factors**. By matching coefficients and solving for **undetermined constants**, you can transform a single complicated expression into multiple components that are **easier to integrate**. Practical examples illustrate how these algebraic techniques lead to solutions involving **logarithmic and rational terms**.
