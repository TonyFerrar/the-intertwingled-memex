# MATH 182 - Calculus II
[Catalog Description](https://catalog.unlv.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=50&coid=251591)

Further applications and techniques of integration including integration by parts, sequences and series, polynomial approximations.  
  
Credits: 4  
Prerequisites: C or better in [MATH 181](https://catalog.unlv.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=50&coid=251590).  
Notes: Fulfills the UNLV General Education [Mathematics Requirement](https://www.unlv.edu/provost/gen-ed/core).
