# Course Contract
## MATH 182: Calculus II for Engineers
### Spring 2026

This course employs a well-researched alternative grading technique called [[Contract Grading]]. The goal of this approach is to focus on what matters (your learning through various efforts) rather than a points-based accounting system that leaves us all [[Points-Based Grading is Confusing|confused]] and [[What Do Grades Incentivize?|motivated askew]]. You'll have a clear idea of exactly what is expected of you, and exactly where you stand at any point in the semester.

Everything you do in this course is graded on a Complete/Needs Revision basis. Class attendance, participation, homework, and midterm exams are graded as a whole (each assignment is marked as Complete/Needs Revision); the Final Exam is graded Complete/Incomplete on a question-by-question basis.

---

Learning in this course is viewed as a progression from [[What Does a C Represent?|foundational understanding (C)]], to [[What Does a B Represent?|proficient application (B)]], to [[What Does an A Represent?|advanced and flexible use of ideas (A)]]. Earning higher grades requires completing additional work and demonstrating higher levels of skill mastery.

## Base (C-Level) Contract
To earn the [[What Does a C Represent?|minimum passing grade]] of C in this course, you must earn a "Complete" grade on:
- [ ] 18/26 [[Lecture Session Completion Contract|Lecture Sessions]]
- [ ] 10/15 [[Discussion Session Completion Contract|Discussion Sessions]]
- [ ] 18/26 [[Homework Assignment Completion Contract|Homework Assignments]]
- [ ] 1/2 [[Midterm Exam Completion Contract|Midterm Exams]]
- [ ] xx/xx questions on the [[Final Exam Completion Contract|Final Exam]]

> [!warning]- Missed Opportunities that Cannot Be Made Up
> Based on the contract requirements listed above, any **one** of the following will automatically place you in the [[#Failsafe Option]]:
> - Missing more than 8 Lecture sessions.
> - Arriving over 5 minutes late to more than 8 Lecture sessions.
> - Failure to complete more than 8 sessions' worksheets.
> - Failure to engage with your table in more than 8 sessions.
> - Failure to complete more than 8 sessions' homework assignments, including write ups
> - Missing more than 5 Discussion sessions.
> - Failure to earn a complete in more than 5 Discussion sessions.
> - Missing both Midterm Exams.

## Failsafe Option:
If you've missed the opportunity to complete sufficient work to satisfy the [[#Base (C-Level) Contract]] (C Grade), then your Final Exam score will determine your entire course grade. The highest possible grade you can earn is a C, by completing the Final Exam at a C-level or higher.
