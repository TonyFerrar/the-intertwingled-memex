
# Final Exam
The Final Exam is a critical piece of any technical course because ultimately, we must be accountable for actually acquiring the essential skills reflected in the course objectives. The final exam is cumulative, representing all core concepts from the course. 

Remember: the purpose of the [[Midterm Exams]] is to prepare you for the Final Exam in a reduced-stakes setting. The Final Exam is where it really counts.

> [!danger] The [[Final Exams|Final Exam]] is the only assessment in this course in which your grade depends on the number of correct answers you find.

## Ways Final Exam is Similar to the Midterm Exams
- **Format:** the layout of the final looks just like the midterms.
- **Style:** the styles and types of questions found on the final exam reflect what you saw on the midterms.
	- *Note: the questions themselves will differ - you won't see the exact questions from the midterm exams on the final.*
- **Time-limited:** you will have a limited amount of time to answer the questions on the final exams (120 minutes).
- **Resources:** you will be allowed to use the same [[Resources Permitted in Exams|resources]] on the final exam as the midterm exams.

## Contract-Graded Final Exam
**The Final Exam is scored based on the number of questions you are able to successfully answer.** As stated in the [[Course Contracts|overall course grading contract]], you must:

- Complete xx/xx questions to be eligible for an A in the course
- Complete xx/xx questions to be eligible for a B in the course
- Complete xx/xx questions to be eligible for a C in the course

> [!warning] If you are unable to complete the C-level requirement
> If you are unable to complete xx/xx questions, the highest grade you can earn in the course is a C-. Your grade will be determined by your final exam score, calculated as a pure average of the number of questions you completed.

### Completing Questions
**On a per-problem basis:** If the main solution process is demonstrated, minor errors do not affect complete/incomplete. You must demonstrate that you are able to solve the problem, but if you make minor errors along the way it will not affect your score.

>[!tip] Opportunity to Reduce Pressure
>If you complete 8 hours of work in Test Review Center prior to the Final Exam, then the grade thresholds will be reduced by xx questions:
>- Complete xx/xx questions to be eligible for an A in the course
>- Complete xx/xx questions to be eligible for a B in the course
>- Complete xx/xx questions to be eligible for a C in the course
>
>There is no need to track or submit this time. Knewton does automatically, and we will check before assigning grades. *Note: if you are pursuing this option, you should ensure that Knewton is properly tracking your time.*
