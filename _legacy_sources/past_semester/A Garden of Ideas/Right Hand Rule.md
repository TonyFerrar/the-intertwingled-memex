
# The Right Hand Rule
A valid coordinate system is one in which each axis is orthogonal to the others, according to the [[Right Hand Rule]]. These coordinate systems are defined in a way that can be represented by your right hand.

We tend to use Configuration 2 most often.
# Steps for using the right hand rule:
1. Point your index finger in the **positive $x$ direction** (You can point in any direction you need).
2. Make a $90^{\circ}$ angle between your middle finger and your index finger. Your middle finger will represent the **positive $y$ direction**. (You can orient the direction your middle finger is pointing to which direction you need, just as long as you maintain the $90^{\circ}$ angle).
3. With your index and middle fingers describing the **positive $x$ and $y$ directions**, point your thumb straight out. Your thumb represents which way points in the **positive $z$ direction.**
