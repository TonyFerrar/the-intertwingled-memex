
# Midterm Exams
The purpose of a Midterm Exam is to assess your current level of mastery in an exam-like environment. Think of them as graded practice for the [[Final Exams|Final Exam]], with reduced stakes. Rather than scoring these based on the percentage of questions you answer correctly, these exams are graded based on the efforts you invest. 

> [!danger] The [[Final Exams|Final Exam]] is the only assessment in this course in which your grade depends on the number of correct answers you find.

## Ways Midterm Exams Are Similar to the Final Exam
- **Format:** the layout of the midterm exams looks just like the final.
- **Style:** the styles and types of questions found on the midterm exams reflect what you will see on the final. 
	- *Note: the questions themselves will differ - you won't see the exact questions from the midterm exams on the final.*
- **Time-limited:** you will have a limited amount of time to answer the questions on the midterm exams (75 minutes) as well as the final exam (120 minutes).
- **Resources:** you will be allowed to use the same [[Resources Permitted in Exams|resources]] on the final exam as the midterm exams.
