
> [!question]- What happens if a class/discussion session is cancelled?
> We will update the contract requirements, reducing them by 1.

> [!question]- How do I earn credit?
> We are minimizing the amount of work that you need to submit via Canvas by inspecting your work in person. You will submit completed contracts (signed by instructor or peer mentor) via Canvas assignments.

> [!question]- How do I get a contract signed?
> Once you feel you have satisfied a Contract, bring the materials with you to the next session of our course (Lecture or Discussion). If time permits, we will review it and sign your contract for you. If time does not permit, you have options:
> 1. attend faculty or peer mentor office hours and obtain signatures
> 2. bring your completed work to our next lecture or discussion session
> 3. scan and submit all of your work to Canvas along with the unsigned contract. This is less ideal because it requires you to scan and upload more, which we don't want to waste too much of your time with if we can help it.

> [!question]- When are contracts due?
> To succeed in this course, you will need to make consistent, regular progress. Allowing things to back up week after week will lead to disaster. However, the contracts do not have specific due dates because you may be told to "revise and resubmit." In lieu of deadlines, we have provided you with [[A Week in the Life of Contract Grading]], which lays out a plan that will keep you from falling behind.
