
# Vector Calculus: Differentiation

 Let $f$, $g$, and $h$ be differentiable functions of $t$.
 
 For $\vec{r}(t) = f(t)\hat{i} + g(t)\hat{j} + h(t)\hat{k}$
 
 The derivative is
 
 $$\vec{r}'(t) = f'(t)\hat{i} + g'(t)\hat{j} + h'(t)\hat{k}$$
 
 In other words, treat $f(t)$, $g(t)$, $h(t)$ separately using the standard derivative rules, then combine to form a new vector.

## Tangent Vectors
$\vec{r}'(t)$ is the tangent vector to $\vec{r}(t)$ by definition. What this means is that at any value of $t$, $\vec{r}'(t)$ will point tangent to $\vec{r}(t)$.

### Principal Tangent Vectors
The principal unit tangent vector is a tangent vector with magnitude 1. As with all unit vectors, it is found by dividing by the magnitude:
 $$ \hat{T}(t) = \frac{\vec{r}'(t)}{|\vec{r}'(t)|}$$
