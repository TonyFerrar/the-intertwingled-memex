
# Setting Up and Evaluating Integrals for the Method of Shells

The fundamental skill required for solving volumes by shells is the ability to to accurately translate geometric regions into **definite integrals** for various rotation scenarios. This involves correctly defining the **radius** and **height** when rotating about:

• The **y-axis** ($V = \int{2 \pi x f(x)dx}$).
• The **x-axis** ($V = \int{2 \pi y g(y)dy}$).
• **Arbitrary vertical or horizontal lines** (e.g., $x=L$ or $y=L$), which requires adjusting the radius in the formula to $(x−L)$ or $(y−L)$.
