
# Green's Theorem - Circulation Form

> [!info] Green's Theorem - Circulation Form Essential Formulas
> 
> **Green's Theorem - Circulation Form:**
> $$\oint_C P\,dx + Q\,dy = \iint_R (Q_x - P_y)\, dA.$$

 For a positively oriented, simple closed curve $C$ enclosing region $R$:

$$\oint_C P\,dx + Q\,dy = \iint_R (Q_x - P_y)\, dA.$$

**Interpretation:**  
Left side = total circulation around $C$.  
Right side = net microscopic rotation inside $R$.

> [!success] Step by Step Apply Green's Theorem (Circulation Form)
> 
> To evaluate $\oint_C P\,dx + Q\,dy$:
> 
> 1. Compute $Q_x - P_y$.
> 2. Sketch the region $R$.
> 3. Set up the double integral:
> $$\iint_R (Q_x - P_y)\, dA.$$
> 4. Choose the easiest order of integration.
> 5. Compute the integral.
