# Triple Integrals over General Regions


> [!info] Triple Integrals over General Regions Essential Formulas
>**Triple Integrals over General Regions:**
>$$\iiint_E f(x,y,z)\,dV = \int_{x=a}^{b} \int_{y=g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)} \int_{z=h_1(x,y)}^{h_2(x,y)} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dy\,dx$$
>$$z\in[h_1(x,y),\,h_2],\quad y\in[g_1(x),\,g_2(x)],\quad x\in[a,\,b]$$

 Just like with [[Triple Integrals over Rectangular Regions]], the idea behind doing a triple integral over a general region hasn't changed much when jumping from [[Double Integrals over a General Region|double]] to triple integrals. The main thing is that these problems now may give you bounds such as
 $$z\in[h_1(x,y),\,h_2],\quad y\in[g_1(x),\,g_2(x)],\quad x\in[a,\,b]$$
 These bounds tend to result in a triple integral such as 
 $$\iiint_E f(x,y,z)\,dV
= \int_{x=a}^{b} \int_{y=g_1(x)}^{g_2(x)}
\int_{z=h_1(x,y)}^{h_2(x,y)} f(x,y,z)\,dz\,dy\,dx
$$
