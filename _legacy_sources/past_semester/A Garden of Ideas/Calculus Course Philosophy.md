
# Calculus Course Philosophy

## The Student Experience
A lot of students come into calculus carrying fear: 

>“This class is too hard.” 

>“I’m just not a math person.”

My goal is to flip that script. **I want you to feel challenged but supported**, and to leave each class a little stronger and more confident than when you walked in.

## Thinking Like an Engineer
Engineers don’t just crunch numbers—they design systems that work on purpose. To do that, you’ll need to move fluidly between three roles:
1. **Physicist** – Define the system and the governing principles.
2. **Mathematician** – Use calculus tools to solve the model.
3. **Engineer** – Apply the results to design something that works.

Every topic in this course is chosen to strengthen that way of thinking.

## Why It Matters
Math isn’t just about passing exams—it’s a gateway to solving real problems in the world. Engineers have the power to design a future that’s more sustainable, fair, and inspiring. That’s why this course matters: it’s not just about formulas, it’s about giving you the tools to shape the future.
