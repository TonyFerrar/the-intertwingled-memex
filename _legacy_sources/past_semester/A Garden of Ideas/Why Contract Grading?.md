# Why Contract Grading?

In most classes, grades are based on points, where small mistakes can cost you heavily and learning is judged by an evaluator in a single moment. Contract grading focuses on _your effort and follow-through over time_. Instead of earning points, each assignment is marked **Complete** or **Needs Revision**. You’ll have clear opportunities to revise your work until it meets expectations, and your course grade is directly tied to how many assignments you complete. **You are still responsible for mastering the skills in this course, but this system makes it much clearer to check in at any point and identify what you’ve learned, what still needs work, and where you stand.** This approach shifts the focus from avoiding mistakes to doing the work, learning from feedback, and building confident mastery through consistent practice.
