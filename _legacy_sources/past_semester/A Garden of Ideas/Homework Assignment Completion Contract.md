## Math Notation & Solution Writing

In addition, you must:

> [!todo] Develop proper math notation and solution writing. 
> - [ ] Select one problem from each assignment for this session to write up. For each:
> 	- [ ] **Given:** Summarize the information given in the problem statement.
> 		- For word problems, name each value provided in the problem statement with a variable.
> 	- [ ] **Find:** Summarize the results the problem statement is asking for.
> 		- For word problems, name each result asked for in the problem statement with a variable.
> 	- [ ] **Solution:**  Your work to find the answer(s).
> 		- [ ] Use headings for key steps
> 		- [ ] Use white-space and "nested outline layout" to enhance readability
> 		- [ ] Write in pencil (or digital ink), erase mistakes.
> 		- [ ] Use proper notation for all mathematical statements.
> - [ ] Show your writeups to the GA during Discussion. *GA: Initial next to each assignment listed above if it meets meets the requirements listed here.*
> 
> Note: you may revise any write up that does not meet requirements until 11:59pm the night before our next [[Class Schedule| midterm exam or final exam]]. A sample of a write up that would earn a Complete score is [[Sample Solution|available here]].
