
# Course Contract Grade Tracker
This course employs [[Contract Grading]] to ensure you get to focus on what really matters: learning the material and building skills. An added benefit of this approach is that you can keep a very clear picture of where you stand - that's the purpose of this document.

## How To:
As you earn "Complete" grades on each contract in Canvas, check them off on the following pages.

> [!example] Overall Requirements
> |  | C Contract | B Contract | A Contract |
> | --- | --- | --- | --- |
> | Lecture Contracts | 18/26 | 20/26 | 22/26 |
> | Discussion Contracts | 10/15 | 12/15 | 13/15 |
> | Homework Contracts | 18/26 | 20/26 | 22/26 |
> | Midterm Exam Contracts | 1/2 | 2/2 | 2/2 |
> | Transferrable Skills Sets | 0/3 | 2/3 | 3/3 |
> 
> This is everything you need to do to earn a given grade, other than completing the C, B, and A level requirements on the Final Exam.
