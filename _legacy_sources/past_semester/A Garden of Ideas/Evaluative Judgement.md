
# Evaluative Judgement
To self-grade, a student must compare their messy, "in-progress" work against a rubric or a set of ideal solutions. This requires a deep understanding of the standard you are trying to meet.

- **The Skill:** The ability to objectively critique the quality of one's own output without bias.
- **Technical Context:** In math, this is the difference between blindly following an algorithm and knowing if your derivation "looks" logically sound. It builds the internal "sanity check" needed when solving problems that don't have an answer key in the back of the book.
