# Lecture Session Completion [[Contract Grading|Contract]]

To earn a **Complete** for a class session, fill in this form. *Check off tasks as you complete them.*

> [!info] Course and Student Information
> Course:
> Session \# 
> Student's Name:

> [!todo] Tasks to Complete In Class
>  - [ ] Attend Class, arriving no more than 5 minutes late.
> 	 - Your instructor will come to your table to initial here:
>  - [ ] Arrive prepared, with printed handouts or digital copies loaded on your tablet.
>  - [ ] Complete Today's Assigned Problems with your group.
> 	 - *Note: if you do not complete the required problems, you may work on them outside of class before submitting.*
> 	 - Make a list of the required problem numbers listed in class here:
> 	 - 
> - [ ] Engage with your table to learn collaboratively.
> 	- Use technology to support your efforts, not disengage.
> 	- Blend solo work with discussion and collaboration.
> 	- A Peer Mentor or Instructor will come to your table to initial here: <br><br>
> 
> In short, show up and play along. "Checking out" while present will not earn you credit.

*Keep in mind that some things in life cannot be made up if you miss them. Lectures and collaborative work are two examples - they happen in the moment, never to return. That is why this course [[Course Contracts|does not require completing every session]] to earn the grade you desire.*
