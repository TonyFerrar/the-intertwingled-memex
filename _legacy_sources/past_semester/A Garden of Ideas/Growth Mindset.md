
# Growth Mindset

Growth Mindset is a concept that Carol Dweck, of Stanford University has researched for several decades. Dweck shows that having a growth mindset is the single most predictive measure of whether someone will succeed at learning. The crux of the idea is to shift our thinking from "pass or fail" to "success, or not yet." At its core, Dweck's research shows that if a student believes they can increase their intelligence and ability to grapple with complicated ideas then they will be able to. On the other hand, if someone has a fixed mindset they limit their ability to learn or build their intelligence. The brain is capable of becoming smarter, but only if the individual first believes this to be true.
