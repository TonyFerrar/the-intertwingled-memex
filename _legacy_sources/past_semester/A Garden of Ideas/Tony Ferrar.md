
# Tony Ferrar: About Me


## My Background
I grew up fascinated by how things work, which led me to study **Mechanical Engineering at Virginia Tech**—where I earned all three of my degrees (B.S., M.S., and Ph.D.). My graduate work focused on **jet engines and complex mechanical systems**, and I still geek out whenever I hear that turbine whine overhead.

I've been a professor in the Howard R. Hughes College of Engineering at UNLV since 2019. Before joining the UNLV faculty, I served as a professor in the Mechanical Engineering Department at Temple University from 2016 - 2019.

## Life Outside the Classroom
I live in Las Vegas with my wife, Nicole, and our kids. Family is at the center of everything I do. When I’m not teaching or chasing our boys, I’m usually outside:
- **Rock climbing** (sport, trad, bouldering—you name it)
- Exploring the desert and mountains around Vegas
- Talking anyone’s ear off about engineering entrepreneurship, math, and teaching.

## Why I Teach
I’m here because I believe **engineering school should open doors, not close them**. Too often, students come into math and science courses with fear or doubt. My mission is to change that. I want my courses to be a place where:
- You build real confidence in your problem-solving skills 
- You see how the math connects directly to engineering and design
- You feel supported not just as a student, but as a whole person with a life outside of class

At the end of the day, I’m motivated by a simple idea: **you can do hard things, and I get to help prove it to you.**
