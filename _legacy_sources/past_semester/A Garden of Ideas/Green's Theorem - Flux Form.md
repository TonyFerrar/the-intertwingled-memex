
# Green's Theorem - Flux Form

> [!info] Green's Theorem - Flux Form Essential Formulas
> 
> **Green's Theorem: Flux Form**
> $$\oint_C \mathbf{F}\cdot \mathbf{n}\,ds = \oint_C P\,dy -
> Q\,dx=\iint_R (P_x + Q_y)\, dA.$$


 Outward flux across $C$:

$$\oint_C \mathbf{F}\cdot \mathbf{n}\,ds = \oint_C P\,dy - Q\,dx.$$

Flux form:

$$\oint_C P\,dy - Q\,dx = \iint_R (P_x + Q_y)\, dA.$$

**Interpretation:**  
Left side = total outward flow across the boundary.  
Right side = divergence inside $R$.

### Steps to Solve for Flux

1. Compute $P_x + Q_y$.
2. Sketch the region $R$.
3. Set up:
   $$\iint_R (P_x + Q_y)\, dA.$$
4. Evaluate the integral.
