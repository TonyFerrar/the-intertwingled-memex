
# A Week in the Life: Contract Grading Edition
We're using [[Contract Grading]] to emphasize the value of your effort, and to give you a very clear picture of how you are doing in the course - and what you need to get done to earn the grade you're after.

The purpose of this page is to clarify the process. 

> [!danger] Don't Forget the FAQ Page!
> Remember, we have a [[Contract Grading FAQ]] page to refer to as well.

## Course Landing Pages
Everything you need to know about your course can be found on your Course Landing Page:

> [!success] Spring 2026 Landing Pages
> - [[MATH 182 - Calculus II for Engineers]]
> - [[MATH 283 - Calculus III for Engineers]]

Our class meets Monday, Wednesday, Friday. The following is my suggestion on how to operate:

---

## Sunday
By Sunday night, you should:
- [ ] Review Monday's session page, accessed via your [[#Course Landing Pages|Course Landing Page]].
	- [ ] Print a copy of the [[Lecture Session Completion Contract]]
	- [ ] Print a copy of the [[Homework Assignment Completion Contract]]
	- [ ] Print a copy of the session's handouts

## Monday
---
### In Class:
- [ ] Attend class, earning 2 signatures on your [[Lecture Session Completion Contract]]. One indicates that you attended on time, the other indicates that you participated.
- [ ] Copy the list of required problems from the session page to your [[Lecture Session Completion Contract]].
- [ ] Work with your table to complete the list of required problems.
- [ ] Copy the list of Knewton Assignments for this session onto your [[Homework Assignment Completion Contract]].

### Outside of Class:
- [ ] Begin the Knewton Assignments for this session.

## Tuesday
---
By Tuesday night you should:
- [ ] Complete the Knewton Assignments for this session. By either:
	- [ ] Earning 70% mastery score on all Knewton Assignments
	- [ ] Investing 4 hours distributed evenly among all Knewton Assignments
- *Note: as long as you begin each assignment and make progress, you may continue working after the stated due date to increase your score or time.*

- [ ] Review Wednesday's session page, accessed via your [[#Course Landing Pages|Course Landing Page]].
	- [ ] Print a copy of the [[Lecture Session Completion Contract]]
	- [ ] Print a copy of the [[Homework Assignment Completion Contract]]
	- [ ] Print a copy of the session's handouts

## Wednesday
---
### In Class:
- [ ] Attend class, earning 2 signatures on your [[Lecture Session Completion Contract]]. One indicates that you attended on time, the other indicates that you participated.
- [ ] Copy the list of required problems from the session page to your [[Lecture Session Completion Contract]].
- [ ] Work with your table to complete the list of required problems.
- [ ] Copy the list of Knewton Assignments for this session onto your [[Homework Assignment Completion Contract]].

### Outside of Class:
- [ ] Begin the Knewton Assignments for this session.

## Thursday
---
By Thursday night you should:
- [ ] Complete the Knewton Assignments for this session. By either:
	- [ ] Earning 70% mastery score on all Knewton Assignments
	- [ ] Investing 4 hours distributed evenly among all Knewton Assignments
- *Note: as long as you begin each assignment and make progress, you may continue working after the stated due date to increase your score or time.*

- [ ] Select 2 problems to write up
	- [ ] Write up 1 problem from Monday's homework
	- [ ] Write up 1 problem from Wednesday's homework
- *Note: these can be completed at any point. But you won't earn a "Complete" on a [[Homework Assignment Completion Contract]] until they are done. Keeping up with these is the best way to succeed - but you can do them later if needed.*

- [ ] Print a copy of the [[Discussion Session Completion Contract]].

## Friday
---

### In Class
- [ ] Attend Discussion, earning a signature from your Discussion instructor indicating you performed the required tasks on the [[Discussion Session Completion Contract]].
- [ ] Show your completed handouts from lecture to your Discussion instructor to earn signatures on your [[Lecture Session Completion Contract|Lecture Session Completion Contracts]].
- [ ] Show your 2 written problems to your Discussion instructor, either receiving feedback on what to revise, or a signature on your [[Homework Assignment Completion Contract]].

> [!question] What if we don't have time for all these signatures?
> If time does not permit review of you work, that is OK. We don't want to turn any class session into an in-person grading session. We want to stay focused on value-added learning actions. Hold on to your work and bring it to:
> - your next lecture or discussion
> - office hours (Dr. Ferrar or Peer Mentors)
> - Last resort: scan and upload all of your work to the appropriate assignment on Canvas. You may choose to do this for any/all assignments, it just takes longer to scan and upload and we want to save you that time if possible.

### Out of Class: Submit Completed Contracts on Canvas
*Completed Contracts may be submitted at any time. Doing this once a week is recommended. If you did not receive signatures during class, you can obtain them during a future class session, or office hours.*

Submit completed contracts only, you do not need to scan the rest of your work - that's why we do the signatures. *If time does not permit obtaining signatures, you may choose to wait or upload all of your work.*

- [ ] Submit any completed [[Lecture Session Completion Contract|Lecture Session Completion Contracts]] to the appropriate assignment on Canvas
- [ ] Submit any completed [[Homework Assignment Completion Contract|Homework Assignment Completion Contracts]] to the appropriate assignment on Canvas
- [ ] Submit any completed [[Discussion Session Completion Contract|Discussion Session Completion Contracts]] to the appropriate assignment on Canvas
