# Finding the Distance Between a Point and a Line


> [!info] Essential Formulas
>**Distance Between a Point and a Line**
>For a point $P$ on the line and point $M$ anywhere in space, the distance between $M$ and $P$ can be found using
>$$d = \dfrac{||\vec{PM}\times\vec{v}||}{||\vec{v}||}$$
>where $\vec{v}$ is a vector along the line and $\vec{PM}$ is the vector pointing from $P$ to $M$.

In order to determine the distance between a point $M$ and a given line $L$, we must establish a point that lies along the line $P$ that ensures that the line $MP$ is perpendicular to $L$.

Since vectors $\vec{PM}$ and $\vec{v}$ form two sides of a parallelogram with area $||\vec{PM}\times\vec{v}||$  parallelogram can also be determined to have an area of $||\vec{v}||d$. Therefore, we have an expression for the distance between a point and a line.
$$d = \dfrac{||\vec{PM}\times\vec{v}||}{||\vec{v}||}$$
