
# New to Canvas?

First off, Canvas (and all learning management systems) is constrained by the ideas of the people who developed it. In some ways, they did a great job. In others, they've failed to understand their customers' needs - especially when it comes to flexibility in grading and content layout.

In my opinion, Canvas is not a fun place to hang out: it's where you go to get bad news:
- A grade...
- An assignment...
- An exam...

We'll spend as little time in Canvas this semester as possible - that's why I created this site. However, your grades, assignments, and deadlines will all be communicated via Canvas.

If this is your first time taking a class in Canvas, don’t worry - I designed our course shell to be straightforward. Here’s a quick start guide:

## Two Ways to View Assignments
- **By Due Date:** Canvas shows upcoming assignments on your dashboard and in the calendar. This helps you see _what’s coming next_ and manage your week.
- **By Type (Recommended):** In our course, I’ve organized assignments by category (Homework, Lecture, Exams, etc.). Viewing by type makes it easier to understand the _purpose_ of each assignment and see how they connect to your learning goals.

## Where to Find Everything

- **Course Materials:** I have migrated all course materials to this site, outside of Canvas. You will find everything you need here, by accessing your course's landing page.
- **Assignments:** The best way to keep up with your assignments is through the _Assignments_ tab. You’ll find everything in order if you view by Type or Due Date.
- **Announcements:** I’ll post updates via Canvas Announcements. Be sure your Canvas notifications are turned on so you don’t miss anything important.
- **Grades:** This is where you can track your progress. You’ll see feedback and scores as they’re posted. Keep in mind that we use [[Course Contracts|Contract Grading]] to make it even simpler to track your progress. I recommend updating your [[Contract Grading Tracker Sheet]] for the easiest view of your current status.

## Quick Tips
- Check Canvas regularly (daily is best).
- Use the Canvas mobile app for quick access, but do your real work on a computer.
- Bookmark our course homepage in your browser.

Canvas is a tool to keep you organized and on track - lean into it, and it will make your semester smoother.
