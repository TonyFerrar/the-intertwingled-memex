You develop the skill of using the FTC to evaluate definite integrals as a more efficient alternative to Riemann sums. This involves finding an **antiderivative** ($F(x)$ of a given function $f(x)$) and calculating the **total change** by subtracting the value at the lower limit from the value at the upper limit ($F(b)−F(a)$).
