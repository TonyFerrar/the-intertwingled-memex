# Welcome to Calculus - Engineering Style

Welcome! I am Tony Ferrar, from the Department of Applied and Interdisciplinary Engineering at UNLV, and I'm thrilled that you've chosen to register for this engineering-focused version of Calculus. This course will teach you everything you need from a traditional Calculus course, but we'll do it with an eye for ways that engineers use this math every day in the real world.

We're going to have a great semester!!!!

## What Makes This Class Different

This isn’t just a math class. You’ll use calculus the way engineers do:
- **Build models** (like a physicist)
- **Solve models** (like a mathematician)
- **Use models** (like an engineer)

Every session is active, collaborative, and focused on real problems.

## You’re Not Alone
A team of **Peer Mentors** - students who’ve recently been where you are - will be in class with us. They’ll:
- Guide discussions
- Answer questions
- Share strategies that worked for them

Together, we’ve built a course that’s designed to be one of the **premier learning experiences** in the College of Engineering.

## What to Expect
- Short micro-lectures to set the stage
- Hands-on group problem solving
- Engineering applications every step of the way

Our goal: help you move from _“this looks impossible”_ to _“I didn’t think I could do that… but I did.”_
