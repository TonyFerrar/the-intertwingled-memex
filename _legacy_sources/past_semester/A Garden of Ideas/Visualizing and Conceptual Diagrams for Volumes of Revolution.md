
# Visualizing and Conceptual Diagrams for Volumes of Revolution

Finding volumes of revolution requires the ability to visualize a **solid of revolution** as a collection of infinite, concentric **cylindrical shells**. You must be able to "unroll" these shells into rectangular plates to understand the volume as the product of **circumference** ($2 \pi r$), **height** ($f(x)$), and **thickness ($\Delta x$). You also need to be able to describe what the resulting 3D solid looks like after a rotation.
