

# Adapting Calculations to Different Axes and Variables


This course places a heavy emphasis on the flexibility to work with functions defined in terms of either $x$ or $y$. This involves:

- **Variable Switching:** Recognizing when a curve is defined as $g(y)$ and correctly applying the corresponding theorem to integrate with respect to $y$.
- **Axis of Revolution:** Determining the correct formula based on whether a curve is revolved around the **$x$-axis** or the **$y$-axis**.
- **Problem Analysis:** Deciding which variable of integration is most efficient for a given curve, such as using $x=g(y)$​ over a specific $y$-interval.
