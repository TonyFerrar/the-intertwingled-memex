You learn to utilize various **properties of definite integrals**, such as pulling out constants, reversing limits of integration, and splitting integrals across adjacent intervals. Furthermore, the handouts require you to evaluate more complex integrals using the **substitution method** ($u$ and $du$), ensuring the relationship between the derivative and the integrand is clearly identified.
