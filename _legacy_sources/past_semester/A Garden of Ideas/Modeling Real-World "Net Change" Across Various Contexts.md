
# Modeling Real-World "Net Change" Across Various Contexts


A major goal is the **generalization of calculus concepts** beyond simple physics (velocity and position) to various real-world scenarios. You can explore how to:

- Calculate **population growth** by integrating an annual rate of growth and adding it to an initial population.
- Determine **volume changes**, such as how much water has flowed into a tank or how much oil has leaked from a container over a specific timeframe.

**Note:**
Provide **complete sentence answers with appropriate units**, demonstrating your ability to interpret mathematical results in a practical context.
