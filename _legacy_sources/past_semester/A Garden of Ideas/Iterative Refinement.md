
# Iterative Refinement
The "revise and resubmit" phase breaks the "one-and-done" mentality that plagues many students. In engineering, the first design is almost never the final one.

- **The Skill:** Developing a **growth mindset** specifically applied to technical documentation and problem-solving.
- **The Process:**
    1. **Identify:** Locate the logic break.
    2. **Deconstruct:** Take apart the failed solution.
    3. **Synthesize:** Rebuild the solution with new insights.
- **Engineering Impact:** This mimics the **Engineering Design Process**, where failure is treated as a data point rather than a final grade.
