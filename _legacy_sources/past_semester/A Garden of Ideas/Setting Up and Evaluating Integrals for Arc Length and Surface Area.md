
# Setting Up and Evaluating Integrals for Arc Length and Surface Area

The primary technical skill developed in this session is the ability to translate the geometric properties of a curve into a solvable definite integral.

- **Arc Length:** You must learn to identify the function $f(x)$, calculate its derivative $f′(x)$, and substitute these into the formula 
$$\text{length} = \int_a^b{\sqrt{1+ \left( f'(x)\right)^2}dx}$$


- **Surface Area:** For solids of revolution, you must incorporate the radius of revolution (often $f(x)$ or $f(y)$) into the integral: 

$$\text{area} = \int_a^b{2 \pi f(x) \sqrt{1+ \left( f'(x)\right)^2}dx}$$

**Application:** These problems require not just setting up these integrals, but also evaluating them using techniques like **u-substitution**. We will encounter many advanced techniques for evaluating these integrals in a future unit of the course.
