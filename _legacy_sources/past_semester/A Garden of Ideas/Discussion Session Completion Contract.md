# Discussion Session Completion [[Contract Grading|Contract]]

To earn a **Complete** for a Discussion session, fill in this form. *Check off tasks as you complete them.*

> Course:
> Session \# 
> Student's Name:

> [!todo] Tasks to Complete In Class
>  - [ ] Attend Discussion, arriving no more than 5 minutes late.
>  - [ ] Participate in exercises and activities as directed by your instructor.
>  - Your instructor will come to your table to initial here, indicating you have satisfied both requirements:
> 
> In short, show up and play along. "Checking out" while present will not earn you credit.
