
# Peer Mentor Checklist
If you're on the clock and not working with a student, please run through this list (in order) and accomplish as much as you can:

## Grade Every Submission on Canvas
**Do this for any contract that has been turned in on Canvas but is not yet graded.**

- [ ] Review Canvas submissions and grade any ungraded contracts.

> [!question]- How To
> - Canvas >> Course
> 	- Check the To Do list
> ![[Pasted image 20260128135116.png]]
> 
> - Click an assignment in the todo list to open Speed Grader
> - 
> ![[Pasted image 20260128135228.png]]
> - Review the work, select the grade from the drop down, type a comment to explain any "incomplete."
> 	- Add encouragement when it occurs to you, you know these people from class.
> 
> **If you mark a submission "incomplete" you must provide feedback on what needs to change to earn a "complete" on a resubmission.**


## Remind Students to Submit Completed Contracts
**Do this for any contract from last week (or earlier).** In other words, if students have had a week since the start of a given contract, remind them to complete it and submit to stay on top of the course.

- [ ] Remind students to submit anything over a week old.

> [!question]- How To
> - Use the "message students who... " feature in Canvas gradebook
> 	- Canvas >> Gradebook
> 
> ![[Pasted image 20260128114434.png]]
> 
> - For students who... "Have not yet submitted"
> 
> ![[Pasted image 20260128114531.png]]
> 
Send a gentle reminder for students who have not yet submitted.



## Remind Students to Start
Do this for any Knewton assignment that has been assigned.
- Do this on Tuesday for the assignments from Monday
- Do this on Thursday for the assignments from Tuesday
- Do this again for anything that should have been started last week or earlier

- [ ] Remind students to get started on their homework.

> [!question]- How To
> - Use the email feature in Knewton, for each assignment.
> 	- Knewton >> Course >> Section >> click on an assignment
> 
> ![[Pasted image 20260128115046.png]]
> 
> - Click on the gray bar to filter to students who have Not Started
> 
 ![[Pasted image 20260128115123.png]]
 >
>  -  Use the check box to select all of the filtered students
>
>![[Pasted image 20260128115218.png]]
>
>- Click the "email" button at the bottom of the window.
>
>![[Pasted image 20260128115237.png]]
>
>- You should end up with everyone's names in a single email, but they are all BCC to protect privacy.
>
>**Send a gentle reminder to get started on the assignment and ask if they need anything.**
>
>**- Copy the entire Peer Mentor team in the CC section. That way you all know which assignments have had emails sent.**
>


## Encourage Students Who Struggle
Do this for any Knewton assignment that has been due.

- [ ] Send encouragement, offer of support to anyone who Knewton identifies as struggling

> [!question]- How To
> - Use the email feature in Knewton, for each assignment.
> 	- Knewton >> Course >> Section >> click on an assignment
> 
> ![[Pasted image 20260128115046.png]]
> 
> - Click on the red bar to filter to students who are struggling
> 
 ![[Pasted image 20260128140755.png]]
 >
>  -  Use the check box to select all of the filtered students
>
>![[Pasted image 20260128140922.png]]
>
>- Click the "email" button at the bottom of the window.
>
>![[Pasted image 20260128115237.png]]
>
>- You should end up with everyone's names in a single email, but they are all BCC to protect privacy.
>
>**Send a gentle encouragement, pointing out that Knewton identified them as struggling for one reason or another. Ask if they are doing ok, and whether they would like any support.**
>- Often, Knewton points out people who do not feel they are struggling. ASK, rather than assuming they are having a hard time.
>
>**- Copy the entire Peer Mentor team in the CC section. That way you all know which assignments have had emails sent.**
>
