## Skill 1: Dot Product:

> [!info]- Essential Formulas
> The Dot Product produces a scalar.
>$$ \mathbf{a} \cdot \mathbf{b} = a_x b_x + a_y b_y + a_z b_z $$
>The Dot Product can be used to find the angle between two vectors.
>$a\cdot b=|a||b|\cos\theta$

### Skill 1 Demo: 

Find the dot product of $\vec{u}=\langle6,7\rangle$ and $\vec{v}=\langle7,-6\rangle$.



%%
> [!Success]- Problem S1.1 Solution
> Insert Solutions Here 
> $\mathbf{u}=\langle 6,7\rangle,\;\mathbf{v}=\langle 7,-6\rangle$  
> $\mathbf{u}\cdot\mathbf{v}=6(7)+7(-6)=42-42=0$  
> **Final answer:** $\boxed{0}$

## Skill 2: Cross Product 

> [!info]- Essential Formulas
>The Cross Product.
> $$
> \mathbf{a} \times \mathbf{b}
> =
> \begin{vmatrix}
> \mathbf{i} & \mathbf{j} & \mathbf{k} \\
> a_x & a_y & a_z \\
> b_x & b_y & b_z
> \end{vmatrix}
> $$
> 
> $$=(a_y b_z - a_z by)\hat{i}-(a_x b_z-a_z b_x)\hat{j}+(a_x b_y-a_y b_x)\hat{k} $$
> $$|\vec{a} \times \vec{b}| =|\vec{a}| |\vec{b}| \sin{\theta}$$



### Skill 2 Demo:
Calculate $\vec{p} \times \vec{q}$ using a determinant if $\vec{p}=2\hat{i}+4\hat{j}$ and $\vec{q}=-4\hat{i}-5\hat{j}$.

%%
> [!Success]- Problem S2.1 Solution
> Insert Solutions Here
> $$\vec{p} \times \vec{q}=\begin{vmatrix}\hat{i} & \hat{j} & \hat{k} \\2 & 4 & 0 \\
-4 & -5 & 0\end{vmatrix}$$
> $$\vec{p} \times \vec{q}=\hat{k}\begin{vmatrix}
2 & 4 \\-4 & -5\end{vmatrix}$$
> $$= \hat{k}\big(2(-5) - 4(-4)\big)$$
> $$= \hat{k}(-10 + 16)= 6\hat{k}$$

%%

### Problem S3.1 
Find the work (in Joules) done by the force $\vec{F}=\langle4,-4,4\rangle$ (in Newtons) acting on a particle as it moves from point $P(-7,-2,5)$ to point $Q(8,-7,-5)$ along a straight line. The coordinates of $P$ and $Q$ are measured in meters. Units: 
* $1 \, N = 1 \, kg \cdot m/s^2$
* $1 \, J = 1 \, N \cdot m$

%%
> [!Success]- Problem S3.2 Solution
> Insert Solutions Here
> $\mathbf{F}=\langle 4,-4,4\rangle$  
> $\mathbf{d}=\langle 15,-5,-10\rangle$  
> $W=\mathbf{F}\cdot\mathbf{d}=4(15)+(-4)(-5)+4(-10)=40$  
> **Final answer:** $\boxed{40\ \text{J}}$
