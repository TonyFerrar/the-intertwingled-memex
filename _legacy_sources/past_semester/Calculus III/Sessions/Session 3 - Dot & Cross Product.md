# Session 3 - Dot & Cross Product

## Session Overview
Engineers use the dot and cross products to translate abstract vectors into meaningful system behavior. The **dot product**measures how much one vector aligns with another, letting us calculate quantities like the _work_ a force does along a path or the _angle_ between structural supports. The **cross product** captures perpendicularity and area, producing vectors that represent _torque_, _rotation axes_, or the orientation of a surface. Together, these tools let engineers quantify how forces and motions interact in space, bridging the gap between geometry and real mechanical systems.
