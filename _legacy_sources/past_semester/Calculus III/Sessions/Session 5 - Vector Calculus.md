# Session 5 - Vector Calculus
Motion in Mechanical Systems

## Session Overview
Vector-valued functions let engineers describe and predict motion in space. By differentiating these functions, we obtain velocity and acceleration—quantities that reveal how systems change over time. Mechanical engineers use them to model robotic arms moving with precision; civil engineers apply them to analyze how structures respond to moving loads or vibrations; electrical engineers rely on them for tracking drones or sensor packages in crosswinds; and entertainment engineers design safe, smooth motion paths for stage rigs and ride systems. No matter the field, understanding motion through vector-valued functions equips engineers to design systems that move accurately, efficiently, and safely.
