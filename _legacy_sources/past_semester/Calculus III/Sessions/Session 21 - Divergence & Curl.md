# Session 21 - Divergence & Curl

## Session Overview
Curl and divergence allow engineers to diagnose how a system behaves at its most local level.  
- In **HVAC and aerodynamics**, high curl indicates rotational pockets or vortices that increase drag or disrupt airflow.  
- In **water distribution and piping**, divergence identifies where pressure is building (positive divergence) or collapsing (negative divergence).  
- In **electromagnetics**, divergence connects directly to charge density, while curl relates to how magnetic fields evolve.

Understanding curl and divergence means understanding how energy, fluid, or force behaves in space — a foundational skill for engineering analysis and design.
