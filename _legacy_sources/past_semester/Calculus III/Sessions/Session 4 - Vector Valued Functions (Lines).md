# Session 4 - Vector Valued Functions (Lines)

## Session Overview
In engineering design, lines and planes are more than abstract geometry—they are the language of layout and alignment. Civil engineers model beams, supports, and roadway intersections. Mechanical engineers align parts, analyze contact surfaces, and guide motion paths. Electrical engineers map fields, design circuit layers, and study wave propagation. Entertainment engineers shape stage layouts, lighting angles, and mechanical effects. By mastering how to represent and analyze lines and planes with vectors, students gain tools to solve real problems where orientation and intersection matter. **Whether in infrastructure, machines, signals, or live experiences, precision in 3D space is a challenge shared across engineering.**
