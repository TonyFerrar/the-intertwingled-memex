# Session 17 - Change of Variables in Multiple Integrals

## Session Overview
Engineers often need to compute volumes, fluxes, or energy distributions inside regions with awkward boundaries. By changing variables to match the geometry, we make integrals easier to evaluate and gain deeper physical insight.

Mechanical and civil engineers analyze stress in curved tanks and domes; electrical engineers compute fields around conductors; environmental engineers model diffusion in irregular basins; entertainment engineers map lighting on curved projection surfaces.

Recognizing *symmetry* or *repeating patterns* and choosing the right transformation is an essential design skill.
