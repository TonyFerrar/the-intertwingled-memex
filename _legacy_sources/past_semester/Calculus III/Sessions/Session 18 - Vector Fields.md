# Session 18 - Vector Fields

## Session Overview
Vector fields are everywhere engineers look. They describe how air flows around aircraft wings, how magnetic forces guide electric currents, how water moves through pipes and soil, and how temperature or pollutants spread through a room. Mechanical and civil engineers use vector fields to analyze fluid motion and load distribution; electrical engineers use them to model electric and magnetic fields; environmental engineers interpret air circulation and diffusion; and entertainment engineers harness vector fields to create immersive motion, light, and
sound effects.

Across every discipline, vector fields transform invisible forces into visible, measurable patterns that can be modeled, optimized, and ultimately engineered into real systems.
