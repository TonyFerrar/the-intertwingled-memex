# Session 23 - Surface Integrals

## Session Overview
Surface integrals show up any time engineers care about what crosses a boundary in 3D space. Mechanical and civil engineers use them to measure how much force, pressure, or stress pushes through a panel, beam, or shell so they can design structures that won’t fail under wind, water, or load. Environmental and civil engineers use flux across surfaces to track how pollutants, groundwater, or heat move through soil layers, riverbeds, or building envelopes. Electrical and entertainment engineers rely on surface integrals to quantify how electromagnetic fields pass through antennas, sensor arrays, and even curved stage elements, helping them shape signal strength, reduce interference, and safely deliver power. In all of these cases, surface integrals turn “stuff flowing through a surface” into a precise number that can drive design decisions.
