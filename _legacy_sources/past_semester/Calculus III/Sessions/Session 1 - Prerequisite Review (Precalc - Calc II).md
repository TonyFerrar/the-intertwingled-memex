# Session 1 - Welcome + Prerequisite Review

## Session Overview
Welcome to Calculus III (for engineers)! I am [[Tony Ferrar]], your instructor. Today, we'll discuss the course itself to be sure you know exactly what you need to do in order to thrive while accomplishing your goals. We'll also break into groups and work through a handout which guides your review of the essential math skills you learned in [[Precalculus I]], [[Precalculus II]], [[Calculus I]], and [[MATH 182 - Calculus II for Engineers|Calculus II]]
