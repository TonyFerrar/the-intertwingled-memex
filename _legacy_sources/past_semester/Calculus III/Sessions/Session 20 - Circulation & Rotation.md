# Session 20 - Circulation & Rotation

## Session Overview
Understanding conservative fields and Green’s Theorem gives engineers a powerful way to analyze how quantities move, rotate, or circulate in a 2D system.

Environmental and civil engineers use these tools to model water flow around barriers or pressure circulating in a basin. Mechanical engineers examine rotating components by studying how vector fields swirl around a region. Electrical engineers analyze planar current flow or predict whether a field can be described by a potential function. Entertainment engineering and design students interpret how simulated forces move in a 2D environment.

In today’s session, we connect the behavior of a vector field **along a boundary** to what is happening **inside a region**, building the foundation for curl, divergence, and full 3D field analysis in the next session.
