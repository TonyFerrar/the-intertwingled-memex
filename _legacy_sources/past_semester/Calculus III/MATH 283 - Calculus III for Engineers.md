# MATH 283 - Calculus III
[Catalog Description](https://catalog.unlv.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=50&coid=251596)

Vectors; differentiation and integration of vector valued functions; multivariable calculus; partial derivatives; multiple integrals and applications; line, surface and volume integrals; Green’s theorem; divergence theorem; and Stoke’s theorem.  
  
Credits: 4  
Prerequisites: C or better in [MATH 182](https://catalog.unlv.edu/preview_course_nopop.php?catoid=50&coid=251591). 

Notes: Fulfills the UNLV General Education [Mathematics Requirement](https://www.unlv.edu/provost/gen-ed/core).
