# Calculus Course Weekly Themes

This document contains a week-by-week theme outline for Calculus 1, Calculus 2, and Calculus 3 based on the courses' active learning sessions and discussion sheets.

---

## [[Courses/Calc-1/index|Calc 1]]
- **Week 1**: Functions and Introduction to Limits
- **Week 2**: Algebraic Techniques for Limits and Asymptotes
- **Week 3**: Continuity, Special Limits, and Rates of Change
- **Week 4**: Basic Derivatives and Differentiation Rules
- **Week 5**: Advanced Differentiation Techniques and Midterm 1 Review
- **Week 6**: Related Rates and Indeterminate Forms
- **Week 7**: Extrema and Optimization of Geometry
- **Week 8**: Optimization of Motion and Curve Sketching
- **Week 9**: Numerical Methods and Introduction to Vectors
- **Week 10**: Vector Operations, Applications, and Midterm 2 Review
- **Week 11**: Introduction to Integration and Substitution Rule
- **Week 12**: Definite Integrals and the Fundamental Theorem of Calculus
- **Week 13**: Final Assessments and Comprehensive Review

---

## [[Courses/Calc-2/index|Calc 2]]
- **Week 1**: Calculus I Review and Area under a Curve
- **Week 2**: Approximating Integrals and $u$-Substitution
- **Week 3**: Net Change and Volumes by Slicing
- **Week 4**: Volumes of Revolution (Shell Method) and Geometric Applications
- **Week 5**: Engineering/Physical Applications and Midterm 1 Review
- **Week 6**: Numerical Methods and Matrices in Engineering
- **Week 7**: Numerical Integration and Integration by Parts
- **Week 8**: Trigonometric Integrals and Trigonometric Substitution
- **Week 9**: Partial Fractions Decomposition and Improper Integrals
- **Week 10**: Sequences, Infinite Series, and Midterm 2 Review
- **Week 11**: Series Convergence Tests and Power Series
- **Week 12**: Parametric Curves and Polar Coordinates
- **Week 13**: Comprehensive Final Exam Review

---

## [[Courses/Calc-3/index|Calc 3]]
- **Week 1**: Welcome, Prerequisite Review, and Introduction to Vectors in 2D & 3D
- **Week 2**: Vector Operations (Dot & Cross Products) and Lines & Planes in Space
- **Week 3**: Vector-Valued Functions, Space Curves, Arc Length, and Curvature
- **Week 4**: Midterm 1 Review, Curvature Review, and Introduction to Multivariable Functions
- **Week 5**: Domain/Range of Multivariable Functions, Partial Derivatives, and Linear Approximations
- **Week 6**: Multivariable Chain Rule, Higher Derivatives, and Gradients
- **Week 7**: Constrained Optimization (Lagrange Multipliers) and Double Integrals
- **Week 8**: Double Integrals in Polar Coordinates and Triple Integrals in Rectangular Coordinates
- **Week 9**: Coordinate Transformations (Cylindrical, Spherical, Jacobian) and Multiple Integrals Applications
- **Week 10**: Midterm 2 Review and Introduction to Vector Fields
- **Week 11**: Line Integrals and Introduction to Vector Calculus Theorems (Green's, Curl, Divergence)
- **Week 12**: Curl & Divergence in Physical Systems and Surface Integrals
- **Week 13**: Comprehensive Final Exam Review
